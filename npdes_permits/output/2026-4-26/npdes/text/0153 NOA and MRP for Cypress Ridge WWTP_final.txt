Attachment 1 - 3 - Treatment Solids removal, nitrification, denitrification, and organic
material removal occur in two sequencing batch reactors
(U.S. Filter Jet Tech Omnifo SBR’s) with a total capacity of
140,00 gpd. Treated effluent is transferred to a 18,500 gallon
decant tank, and settled sludge is pumped to a 34,000 gallon
digester tank. The water receives chemical dosing (Blue &
White A1N30F-4T) and rapid mixing (Komax – Custom
Polymer Static Mixer) in a 2,100 gallon flocculation tank.
Sand filtration is provided by a dual U.S Ftler, Zimpro Hydro-
Clear San Filter Model 714.
Disinfection Effluent enters the chlorine contact chamber where a SCADA
controlled chlorine feed pump adds a 12.5% sodium
hypochlorite solution. A free chlorine analyzer (Hach 9184
SC) sends the chlorine level to the SCADA system for dosing
rates. Then the water traverses through 2 PVC piping routes
(84 minute and 15 minute detention times). At this point, a
free chlorine analyzer (Hach CL17) reads the residual
chlorine and then the effluent continues through a third PVC
pipe (11 minute detention time).
Effluent Storage There is one 3.1 million gallon unlined emergency storage
pond
Disposal Effluent is treated to disinfected tertiary recycled water
standards and is stored in a lined primary impoundment
(Figures 2 and 3) on the Cypress Ridge Golf Course. If the
primary impoundment reaches capacity, Cypress Ridge Golf
Course pumps water to a secondary impoundment (Figures
2 and 3). Regulatory coverage for the use of recycled water
is authorized by a separate Order.
