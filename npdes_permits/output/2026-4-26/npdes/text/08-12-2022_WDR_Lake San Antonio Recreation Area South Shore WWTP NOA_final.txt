Primary Treatment A 50,000 gallon digestor tank removes settleable solids.
Secondary Treatment Primary effluent enters two facultative ponds that can be
operated in series or parallel. When th ere is sufficent flow,
facultative pond effluent from pond 1 can be diverted to the
trickling filter for additional treatment before being
recirculated to pond 2.
Disposal The facility was designed to dispose of effluent via onsite
spray irrigation field, but currently percolates and
evaporates effluent in their unlined effluent disposal pond
(Pond 3) . The system is permitted to discharge to Pond 3,
if the flow increases and spray disposal is needed, the
County of Monterey may request a change via writing to
Central Coast Water Board staff for review and approval.
