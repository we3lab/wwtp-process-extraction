2. WASTEWATER SYSTEM OPERATION SUMMARY

Los Alamos Wastewater Treatment Plant wastewater treatment facility (Wastewater
System) is a Class I wastewater system. Los Alamos Community Services District must
staff a Grade I chief plant operator for proper operation of the Wastewater System. The
current chief plant operator is Juan Ramon Gomez , Grade 3, License Number 106 93.

In the notice of intent, Los Alamos Community Services District proposes that the Wastewater System is designed to treat and dispose of 200,000 gallons per day to a
secondary treatment level. The Wastewater System consists of the treatment
technologies and treatment capabilities shown in Table 2. The Central Coast Water
Board is permitting the operation and disposal of the system as described below. Los Alamos Community Services District must not change operation or disposal technology without Central Coast Water Board notification and review.

Table 2 . Treatment Technology and Capacity

bypass bar screen.

pond where it undergoes aerobic processing of organic substances near the surface. Settled sludges undergo
anaerobic fermentation in the anaerobic lower region of
the pond. Effluent is pumped from Pond 1 into Pond 2, a
facultative pond for further treatment before being land
applied through spray irrigation. Each pond is mechanically equipped with surface aerators. Each pond is
18 feet deep and has a holding capacity of 1.4 million
gallons. The average detention time of seven days.
