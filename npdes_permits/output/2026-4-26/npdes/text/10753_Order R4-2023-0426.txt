BACKGROUND
1. The City of Fillmore (hereafter City) provides domestic wastewater collection,
treatment, and disposal services for the City of Fillmore in Ventura County. The City
owns the Fillmore Water Recycling Plant (Fillmore WRP) located at 1580 River
Street in Fillmore, California (Figure 1) . Fillmore WRP is operated and maintained
by Veolia North America under a contract with the City of Fillmore . The F illmore
WRP discharges disinfected tertiary recycled water to the soil and groundwater via
percolation ponds , a trench disposal system, subsurface perco lation zone s, surface
spray irrigation systems, and subsurface drip irrigation systems within the City of
Fillmore. The City owns a separately regulated sewer collection system.
2. The F illmore WRP was constructed and began treatment operations in September
2009 to replace the Fillmore Wastewater Treatment Plant ( Fillmore WWTP ) that was
built in 1956. The Fillmore WWTP was subsequently decommissioned except for the
percolation ponds after the completion of the Fil lmore WRP .
3. Discharge from the Fillmore WRP was previously regulated under the following
requirements:

and Monitoring and Reporting Program (MRP) No. CI -1076, adopted by the
Los Angeles Water Board on May 11, 2006, regulated effluent discharged to
percolation ponds and effluent as recycled water for Title 22 non -potable
applications .
