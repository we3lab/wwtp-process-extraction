2.1.2. Wastewater Treatment Facili ty
The treatment system consists of a 300,000 -gallon oxidation ditch, secondary
clarifier, chlorine disinfection using calcium hypochlorite tablets, and
dechlorination using sodium bisulfite. The Facility discharges treated wastewater
to three percolation ponds at Discharge Point 002. The percolation ponds are
located in an upland area on property adjacent to the Facility across a deep
ravine. Treated wastewater is conveyed to the percolation ponds via a
suspended transmission line .
2.1.3. Biosolids
Solids removed from the secondary clarifier are dewatered in the Facility’s
sludge drying beds or an Aeromod Draimad bagger before being transported to
an approved landfill for final disposal. Decant water is returned to the inlet
structure prior to the oxidation ditch . Solids were previously disposed of though
land application in a wooded area owned by the Permittee north of the Facility ;
this practice was discontinued in 2018.
