Headworks/Preliminary
Treatment The headworks includes a bar screen and Parkson Aqua
Guard with Screenings Screw Classifier
Headworks capacity (gpd): 2,500,000
Primary Treatment Flow is diverted to two aerated ponds in parallel . The
ponds exhibit approximately 4.1 days and 10-day
hydraulic retention time for the anaerobic pit and aerated
zone, respectively. Following primary treatment, flow is
mixed between the two ponds, then diverted to two
secondary treatment ponds.
Primary ponds treatment capacity (gpd): 600,000
Secondary Treatment Wastewater is directed into two sets of secondary aerated
ponds, operated in parallel. Each pond includes total
digester volume of 110,000 gallons and an aerated
treatment volume of 1,190,000 gallons. Following this,
wastewater is directed to three small p olishing ponds in
series, each with approximately 700,000 gallons of treatment volume, then the two larger polishing ponds in series, with a total volume of approximately 9 million
gallons.
Secondary ponds treatment capacity (gpd): 600,000
Effluent Storage /Wet
Weather Storage The system includes one effluent and wet -weather
storage pond that provides approximately 21 million
gallons of operational storage prior to wastewater
disposal.
Wastewater Disposal
Method Treated effluent is disposed of at the Selby Percolation
Site (Figure 2), which consists of seven percolation ponds
with a combined surface area of approximately 4.4 acres.
 As an alternative disposal option, the facility maintains a 25-acre sprayfield (Figure 3) for use when discharge to
the percolation ponds is not feasible and onsite storage is limited .

Disposal capacity (gpd): 600,000
