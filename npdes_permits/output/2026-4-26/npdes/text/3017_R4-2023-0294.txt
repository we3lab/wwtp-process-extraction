49. Assembly Bill 2108, "Water policy: environmental justice: disadvantaged and tribal
communities," has been signed into law as of September 2022. AB 2108 adds
sections 1 89.7 and 13149.2 to the Water Code. Effective January 1, 2023, this law
requires the Water Boards to conduct equitable, culturally relevant outreach when
considering proposed discharges of waste that may have disproportionate impacts
on water quality in di sadvantaged communities or tribal communities. Also, for
certain actions, the Water Boards must adopt findings related to water quality
impacts in disadvantaged or tribal communities and related to environmental justice
concerns.
