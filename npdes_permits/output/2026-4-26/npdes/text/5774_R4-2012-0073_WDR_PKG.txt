IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the Dischargers, Malibu Bay Club, Incorporated and Ventura
Regional Sanitation District, shall be responsible for and shall comply with the following
requirements in C~ll operations and activities at the Malibu Bay Club Wastewater Treatment Plant
(Malibu Bay Club WWTP):
A. INFLUENT LIMITATIONS
1. Waste discharged shall be limited to domestic wastewater only. No commercial
or industrial wastewaters shall be discharged to the system.
2. The maximum daily flow of influent from the collection system to the wastewater
treatment system shall not exceed the maximum design flow of 36,000 gpd.
3. No volatile organic compounds are to be discharged into the Malibu Bay Club
wastewater treatment plant.
