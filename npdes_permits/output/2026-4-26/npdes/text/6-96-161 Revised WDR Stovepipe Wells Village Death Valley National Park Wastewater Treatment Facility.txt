IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the Discharger shall comply with the following:
• I. DISCHARGE SPECIFICATIONS
A. Discharge Limitations
1. The total flow of wastewater to the treatment and disposal facilities during a
24-hour period shall not exceed 0.030 million gallons.
2. All wastewater made available for percolation at the authorized disposal site
shall have a pH of not less than 6.0 pH units nor more than 9.0 pH units.
3. All wastewater made available for percolation at the authorized disposal site
shall have a dissolved oxygen concentration not less than 1.0 mg/I.
