9. Ponds sha 11 be managed to prevent breeding of mosqu i tos. In particular,
a. An erosion control program shall assure that small coves and
irregularities are not created around the perimeter of the water

b. Weeds shall be minimized through control of water depth,

c. Dead algae, vegetation, and debris shall not accumulate on the

10. Treatment and disposal facilities shall be designed, constructed,
operated, and maintained to prevent inundation or washout due to floods
with a 100-year return frequency.
11. The evaporation/percolation ponds shall have sufficient capacity to
accommodate allowable wastewater flow and design seasonal
precipitation. Design seasonal precipitation shall be based on total
annual precipitation using a return period of 100 years, distributed
monthly in accordance with historica l rainfall patterns. Freeboard
shall never be less than two feet (measured vertically).
12. Effluent discharged to the emergency leachfield shall not surface.
