II. WASTEWATER SYSTEM OPERATION SUMMARY

Gonzales Wastewater Treatment Plant wastewater treatment facility (Wastewater
System) is a Class II wastewater system. Gonzales must staff a Class II chief plant
operator for proper operation of the Wastewater System. The current chief plant
operator is Tiffany Twisselmann , Grade 2, License Number 43086 .

The Wastewater System is designed to treat 1,200,000 gpd (3,700,000 peak hourly ) to
a secondary treatment level. The Wastewater System consists of the treatment
technologies and treatment capabilities shown in Table 2.

Table 2. Treatment Technology and Capacity
Emergency

Headworks/Preliminary

channels with two grinders , then a vortex grit chamber to
settle out large inorganic solids from the influent flow.
Headworks capacity (gpd): 4.0 million gpd (MGD ) peak

Primary and Secondary Treatment Six facultative treatment ponds (Ponds 1 to 6) with surface
aerators , followed by two polishing ponds (Ponds 7 and 8)
function to remove Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD 5)
and Total Suspended Solids (TSS) from the influent
wastewater . The ponds are set up as two parallel trains of
four ponds in series, with each train treating about half of
the flow.
Secondary treatment capacity (gpd): 1,200,000 average
daily flow

Emergency Wastewater Disposal (Infiltration Basin and Controlled Flood Disposal Field s) Treated effluent may be discharged into a temporary infiltration basin (Basin D). Gonzales may only discharge
