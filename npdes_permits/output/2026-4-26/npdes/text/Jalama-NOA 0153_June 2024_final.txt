2.1. Waters of the United States
Jalama Creek is a water of the United States. It is navigable and is the nearest
surface water located approximately 200 to 225 feet north of the restaurant and
playground leachfield. Jalama Creek empties out into the Pacific Ocean
approximately 500 feet northwest of the restaurant leachfield. There is a chloride
and sodium Total Maximum Daily Load for this surface water body as presented
here:

https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/centralcoast/water_issues/programs/tmdl/docs/j
alama_creek/
2.2. Groundwater
Jalama Beach does not overlie a groundwater basin as defined by the California
Department of Water Resources Bulletin 118 or the Water Quality Control Plan
for the Central Coastal Basin (Basin Plan).4 Past subsurface investigations
around the Jalama Beach County Park have encountered groundwater at
approximately 12 feet below ground surface at the playground, Area A, and Area B leachfields . The encountered water is presumed to be ocean water underneath
the backshore.
2.3. Pacific Ocean
The Pacific Ocean is west of Jalama Beach County Park. The ocean’s high tide line is approximately 150 feet west of the septic tanks that serve the restaurant, the northwest small restroom, and the southwest large restroom. It is approximately 200 feet wes t of leachfields Area A and Area B. The septic tanks
and leachfileds are within the area called the backshore.
