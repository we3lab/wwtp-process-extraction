BACKGROUND INFORMATION
The Discharger owns and operates the Facility , which is 6,000 feet south- southwest of
the southernmost point of the Friant Dam . The Facility is on Assessor’s Parcel Number
(APN) Nos. 300-180-13 and 300- 180-15, located in Section 7 , Township 11 S, Range

Staff Memorandum
21 E, M ount Diablo Base & Meridian (36.9826 N, 119 .7142 W) (see Attachment s A and
B of the NOA). The Facility is currently regulated by Waste Discharge Requirements
(WDRs) Order No. 85 -255, which was adopted on 27 September 1985. The Facility
treats domestic wastewater produced from approximately 104 wastewater connections
from the nearby Millerton L ake Mobile Home Village. WDRs No. 85-255 specifies a
30-day average daily dry weather flow limitation of 22,500 gallons per day (gpd) .
Attachment C of the NOA is a process flow diagram for the Facility. The Facility is an
extended aeration activated sludg e package plant. The influent passes through a
manual bar screen, then to a wet -well with two influent pumps , and then to the aeration
tank. Soda ash is added to the aeration tank for pH adjustment. The Facility includes a
chlorine contact chamber; however, chlorine is not currently used in the contact chamber since WDRs Order 85- 255 did not require the effluent to be disinfected. The
effluen t is discharged to an unlined percolation/evaporation pond. Since the original
construction, the effluent percolation/evaporation pond was divided into two ponds
(which function now in series) by a dirt berm and an external anaerobic sludge digester
was added . Approximately 1,500 gallons of sludge is pumped from the digester by an
approved hauler approximately every 21 days or sooner as needed.
Fresno County made multiple modifications to the Facility to comply with the new
requirements in the General Order. These changes included, in part:
1. Re-wired the lift station to change the two influent pumps to a
operational /standby configuration, which increased the Facility’s treatment
detention time and allow ed for increased biological treatment.
2. Reconfigur ed the piping /valves to include a recycle line from prior to the aeration
basin back to the lift station (to stir up solids accumulating in the lift station) and
to allow the new flow meter (item 6 below) to receive the entire plant flow.
3. Reconfigur ed the aeration header to increase air flow to the aeration basi n.
4. Install ed a larger soda- ash tank .
5. Modified sludge removal practices .
6. Install ed a flow meter to measure wastewater flows .
7. Upgrade d to digital timers for the Return Activated Sludge/Waste Activated
Sludge ( RAS/WAS) cycles to provide better control of wasting cycles .
