(disinfection will be eliminated, see attached M emorandum ), an effluent storage pond,
6.7 acres of LAAs , and a tailwater control system . When constructed in 1990, the WWTF was
designed for an average flow of 16,000 gallons per day (gpd ). The site plan and process
schematic are shown o n Attachments B and C, respectively, which are incorporated herein.
