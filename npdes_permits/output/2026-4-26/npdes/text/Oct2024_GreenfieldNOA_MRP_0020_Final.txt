 2. WASTEWATER SYSTEM OPERATION AND TREATMENT SUMMARY
Greenfield Wastewater Treatment Plant (Wastewater System) is a Class II wastewater
treatment facility. Greenfield must staff a Grade II chief plant operator for proper
operation of the Wastewater System. The c urrent chief plant operator is Jason Molinari ,
Grade 4, License Number 28290 .
The Wastewater System was originally designed to treat a peak flow of 2,000,000
gallons per day to a secondary treatment level and had an estimated average disposal
capacity of 1,500,000 gpd. Currently, t he Wastewater System is not consistently
achieving secondary treatment level standards , and the disposal capacity has
decreased. Therefore, the current permitted monthly average flow is 1,200,000 gpd.
Greenfield may propose to increase permitted flow once it demonstrates that disposal
capacity at the ponds and RIBs is sufficient to discharge all wastewater entering the system witho ut pond freeboard violations.
 The Wast ewater System consists of the treatment technologies and treatment
capabilities shown in Table 2. The Central Coast Water Board is permitting the
operation and disposal of the system as described below, Greenfield must not change
operation or disposal without Central Coast Water Board approval . Per General Permit
Section III.7., discharge to areas other than the designated percolation ponds and rapid
infiltration beds shown in Figures 2 and 3, and described in Table 2, is prohibited.

Emergency Wastewater Disposal Sprayfields (Temporary) Greenfield may dispose to emergency wastewater

Biosolids/Sludge Production and Handling Sludge from the clarifiers is pumped to the aerobic
digester (30' diameter, 13.5' deep). In 2023, to i ncrease
solids removal, Greenfield rented a belt press , which drys
biosolids from the digestor . The ultimate destination of
biosolids produced is Johnson Canyon Landfill located at
31400 Johnson Canyon Road, Gonzales . Annual
production of biosolids is 100 dry tons.
