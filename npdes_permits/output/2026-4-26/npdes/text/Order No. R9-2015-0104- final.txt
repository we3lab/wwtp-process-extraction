B. Recycled Water Filling Station Uses/Dischar ges. The VCMWD may establish
Recycled Water Fill Station s at the WVRRWF from which customers may transport
recycled water to use sites for the following uses.
 Street sweeping and cleaning of sidewalks and outdoor work areas.
 Dust control, soil compaction, and construction.
 Sewer flushing and pressure testing of newly constr ucted tertiary recycled
water pipelines, sewer force main pipelines, and gas pipelines.
 Irrigation of commercial and residential landscapes, crops, and nursery stock.
 Fire protection.
