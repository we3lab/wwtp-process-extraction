Wastewater Disposal
Method The treated effluent is pumped in an 8” C900 PVC pipe
that is 20’ below the Salinas R iver bottom. There is also a
backup 4” pipe hanging above the river. The effluent
pump has the ability to pump through either of these 2 pipes, depending upon valving. The effluent pump is located at the plant. Wastewater is then land applied via an overhead irrigation system. Public access is prohibited,
and area is bermed.
 Area: 74 acres of usable land (38 acres are used for spray
irrigation and remaining 36 acres are for future expansion)
Soils: Sandy clay
Perched ground water zones beneath disposal area
Disposal capacity (gpd): 750,000 based on pump sizing
and 24 hrs/ day disposal
Biosolids/Sludge Production and Handling Settled (waste) activated sludge is wasted to an aerobic
digester where it further degrades aerobically to a solids concentration of 1.5- 3.5%. Subsequently, the waste
activated sludge is pumped, with the addition of polymer, into a wedge wire type sludge drying bed for further dewatering to an approximate solids concentration of 10%
– 30%.
Annual production of biosolids: 450 tons
The ultimate destination of biosolids produced is Montere y
