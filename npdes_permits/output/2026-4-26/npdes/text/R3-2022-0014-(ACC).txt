FACILITY DESCRIPTION
2.1. Description of Wastewater and Biosolids Treatment and Controls
The Discharger owns and operates a wastewater collection,  treatment , and 
disposal facility , which provides service to the community of Summerland . The 
Facility receives domestic and commercial wastewater and currently serves 
approximately 480 connections .

ATTACHMENT F – FACT SHEET   F-5The collection system is composed of approximately 8.7 miles of sewer main and 3 
lift stations. The existing treatment plant has been in operation since 19 58 and is 
considered a tertiary treatment facility. The treatment system consists of a  grinder 
and bar screen at headworks,  a flow equalization  basin, primary clarifier , two 
activated sludge aeration basins, two secondary clarifiers, a  chlorine contact 
chamb er, anthracite bed filter, and d echlorination. Wastewater is discharged via a 
740-foot ocean outfall at Discharge Point 001 to the Pacific Ocean, a water of the 
United States. The minimum initial dilution ratio of seawater to effluent is 60:1.
Biosolids ar e managed in two aerobic digesters, dewatered by a two -meter belt 
press, and stockpiled in a sludge holding bed. The holding bed contains drains 
which permit leachate to flow back to the head of the liquid treatment train for 
treatment. Biosolids are perio dically removed and hauled by Liberty Composting  to 
its 162 -acre composting facility in Kern County, California. The Discharger 
composts approximately 100 tons of biosolids per year.
