1. Discharge of wastes from any portion of the WWTF and the sanitary sewer system)

prohibited.
B. Discharge Specifications
1. Effective immediately , the average daily dry weather flow6 shall not exceed
80,000 gpd, and the average daily flow7 shall not exceed 120,000 gpd during the
months of November through July, inclusive.

pursuant to Provision F.1.a, the average daily dry weather flow shall not exceed
105,000 gpd; the average daily flow shall not exceed 142,000 gpd during the months
of November through July, inclusive; and the total annual influent flow shall not exceed 48.4 million gallons.

released or discharged, in a concentration or in a mass that causes violation of the
Groundwater Limitations.
4. Wastewater treatment, st orage, and disposal shall not cause pollution or a nuisance
as defined by Section 13050 of the California Water Code (CWC).
