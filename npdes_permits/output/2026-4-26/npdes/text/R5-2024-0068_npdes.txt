FACILITY DESCRIPTION 
The Discharger provides sewerage service for the Town of Discovery Bay  and serves a 
population of approximately 16,000 . The design daily average dry weather flow capacity 
of the Facility is 2.35 MGD . 
A. Description of Wastewater and Biosolids Treatment and Controls  
The treatment system at the Facility includes two plants (Plant 1 and Plant 2).  The 
Discharger completed construction of Facility upgrades during the last permit term . 
With improvements made at  Plant 1 and Plant 2 , the majority of Plant 1 is 
decommissioned, and Plant 2 is the central treatment facility.  The upgraded Facility 
is operational as of June 2023 and consists of the following treatment system:  
Headworks:  The Facility headworks  are located at Plant 1 and 2. Plant 1 is 
equipped with influent pumps  and a mechanical screen and the emergency storage 
lagoon; the purpose of Plant 1 is to convey screened influent flow from Plant 1 to 
Plant 2. Plant 2 contains additional mechanical screens .  
The Emergency Storage Lagoon can be used, if needed,  to capture peak flows in 
excess of the Facility’s peak design capacity of 2.35 mgd. This flow can be routed 
back to the Facility headworks  for treatment after peak influent flows decrease. The 
Emergency Storage Lagoon is constructed of compacted native soils on the sides 
and bottom. Typically,  several inches of percolated groundwater is present in the 
lagoon, that periodically dries out . The Emergency Storage Lagoon elevations are as 
follows:  
a) Top of pond: 95.9  feet 
b) Bottom of pond: varies between 84.5 feet  
Secondary Treatment , Filtration, and Disinfection : The Facility’s secondary 
treatment system consist s of three parallel trains of oxidation ditches with anoxic 
basins, followed by secondary clarifiers . Secondary  effluent is treated using sand 
filters and disinfected via ultraviolet light  prior to discharge t o Old River  via an 
upgraded multi -port diffuser . 
Solids Treatment:  Sludge handling takes place at Plant 2 and consists of an 
aerated clay -lined pond  (referred to as an aerobic digester), three clay lined sludge 
lagoons, three belt filter presses, and four greenhouse solar drying beds. The 
Discharger produces Class A biosolids. A monitoring well network of four  wells 
surrounds the sludge lagoons and aerobic digester to monitoring groundwater 
quality.   
The aerobic digestor basin elevations are as follows:

b) Bottom of pond: 81 feet

Biosolids are stored on site in the covered solar drying bed buildings identified in 
Attachment C on a concrete floor and are hauled to a local landfill once per year.  
The Facility produces approximately 180 dry metric tons of dried biosolids annually. 
Transportation and disposal/reuse of the biosolids is regulated by U.S. EPA under 40 C.F.R. part 503.

D. Compliance Summary  
In June  2021, the Discharger accepted an offer to participate in the Expedited 
Payment Letter Program relating to Administrative Civil Liability (ACL)  R5-2021-
0515 . The ACL was for violations of effluent limitations for total coliform between 1 
January 2018 through 28 February 2021 and totaled $ 6,000 in mandatory minimum 
penalties .
