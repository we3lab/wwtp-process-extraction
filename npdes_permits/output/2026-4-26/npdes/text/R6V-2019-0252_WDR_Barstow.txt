e. Solids Handling – Sludge and scum from the primary clarifiers are pumped to a

reviewing any water quality control plan or waste discharge requirements, or in
connection with any action relating to any plan or requirement authorized by this
division, may investigate the quality of any waters of the state within its region.”

waste discharge requirements (WDRs). Effluent and groundwater monitoring are

Disch arger to collect representative effluent and groundwater samples and
analyze and report the sampling data to evaluate compliance with the Water

objectives and WDRs . These reports will evaluate compliance with Board Order
requirements and ensure the discharge complies with the Basin Plan. Therefore,
the burden, including costs, of these reports bears a reasonable relationship to
