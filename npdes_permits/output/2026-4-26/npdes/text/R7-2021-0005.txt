FACILITY DESCRIPTION
2.A. Discharge of Wastewater Treatment and Control s
The Discharger owns and operates a wastewater collection, treatment , and disposal 
system (Facility) that provides sewerage service to the City of Brawley , to a 
population of approximately 27,000. The wastewater treatment plant has a treatment 
(design)  capacity of 5.9 MGD and is located in the SW ¼ of Section 15, T13S, 
R14E, SBB&M.
The Facility consists of three raw water lift stations, a headworks facility, three wave 
oxidation Biolac® activated sludge treatment units equipped with diffusers that 
provide extended aeration, three secondary clarifiers, an activated sludge pumping 
station, an ultraviolet disinfection system, and solar greenhouse sludge drying 
structures.
Raw wastewater is directed through the plant headworks, which consists of a step 
screen, compactor, vortex grit pump, and a grit separator. Wastewater receives 
extended aeration through the three Biolac® activated sludge treatment system s, 
whi
ch consists of the primary treatment for the wastewater . The Discharger 
conver ted one of the inactive primary clarifiers into a sludge thickening unit and the 
other inactive primary clarifier into a sludge holding tank. Wasted activated sludge is 
thickened in a sludge thickening unit, dewatered in a centrifuge sludge dewatering 
unit, and then dried in two solar greenhouse sludge drying structures. The Facility 
produces Class A biosolids. The Biolac® process operates without primary 
treatment; therefore, no primary sludge is produced during the treatment process.
The Discharger owns and operates the wastewater collection system, whic h 
provides conveyance of raw wastewater to the treatment facility.  The collection 
system contains both combined and separate sanitary sewer lines.
The Discharger indicated on USEPA Form 2A, there are currently three  significant 
industrial user s (Pioneers Memorial Hospital , One World Beef, GloriAnn Farms ) that 
discharge to the Facility . The Discharger’s Pretreatment Program, submitted to the 
Regional Water Board in January 2013 and re -submitted December 23, 2013, 
received approval March 20, 2014 through Resolution R7 -2014 -0020.
2.B. Discharge Points and Receiving Waters
Final effluent is discharged through Discharge Point 001 at Latitude 32° 01’ 20” 
North and Longitude 115° 3 1’ 00” West, to the New River .  The permitted maximum 
daily flow limitati on is equal to the design capacity of the wastewater treatment plant, 
which is 5.9 MGD. The discharge consists of secondary treated wastewater.
