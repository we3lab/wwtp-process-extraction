FACILITY DESCRIPTION
The Discharger owns and operates a wastewater collection, treatment , and disposal 
system (Facility) that provides sewerage service to the City of Westmorland  and 
surrounding areas , which has  a service population of approximately 2,400 . The 
wastewater treatment plant has a treatment (design) capacity of  0.5 MGD  and is 
located in the SW ¼ of Section 4, T13S, R13E, SBB&M24.
2.A. Discharge of Wastewater Treatment and Control s
Flow from the end of the collection system to the treatment plant is gravity -fed. The 
collection system includes one wet well and two pumps with a capacity  of 600 
gpm25/pump.  The Facility maintains another pump as a backup . The headworks 
system includes a sewage grinder, a sewage pump station, a cyclone unit (for grit 
removal), and an influent flow meter. The influent station does not include screening. 
Flow from the headworks is conveyed to an oxida tion ditch for activated sludge 
generation. Following the oxidation ditch, wastewater is directed to a distribution box 
that conveys flow to one of two 28 -foot secondary clarifiers (one clarifier operates for 
approximately 6 months before alternating to th e other clarifier). Following treatment 
in the secondary clarifiers, flow is sent through one of two chlorine contact basins, 
one of which is on stand -by. The Facility disinfects wastewater using sodium 
hypochlorite for chlorination and sodium bisulfite fo r dechlorination. Following 
disinfection, effluent is discharged through Discharge Point 001.  The Facility  does 
not accep t recreational vehicle (RV) waste.

2.B. Discharge Points and Receiving Waters
Final effluent is discharged through Discharge Point 001 at Latitude 33° 02’ 45” 
North and Longitude 115° 37’ 44” West, to Trifolium Drain No. 6 . The permitted 
maximum daily flow limitation is equal to the design capacity of the wastewater 
treatment plant,  which is  0.5 MGD. The discharge consists of secondary treated 
wastewater .
