FACILITY DESCRIPTION
A. Description of Wastewater and Biosolids Treatment and Controls
1. The wastewater treatment system consists of screens,  a grit chamber, a flow -
equalization basin, a four-train biological treatment process (basins with 
anaerobic, anoxic and aerobic sections fol lowed by a membrane bioreactor and 
internal flow recycle), follo wed by four -train reverse osmosis  process, and 
disinfection with ultraviolet light (UV). Disinfected effluent is either pumped to 
recycled water storage tanks and thence to the recycled water distribution 
system, or it drains to a cascade aerator and final discharge to Cooper’s Creek . 
The Facility produces tertiary treated water that complies with requirements 
established in Title 22 of the California Code of Regulations for non -potable water 
reuse. During the term of the previous permit, t he Discharger installed a reverse

B. Discharge Points and Receiving Waters
1. Tertiary treated wastewater is currently discharged to Cooper's Creek, a tributary 
of Marshall Creek, and to Nobel Creek through the discharge points listed in 
Table F -3, below. These creeks are tributaries of San Timoteo Creek. The points 
of delivery of the recycled water are identified as  DP-002 and DP -003. In addition, 
the Discharger may deliver recycled water to the Beaumont -Cherry Valley Water 
District (BCVWD) for delivery of recycled water to various users for irrigation. The 
Discharger may also use recycled water for groundwater recharge in the future. 
Although this Order addresses the use of recycled water for landscape irrigation, 
such as at DP-002 and DP -003, it will need to be reopened, or the Regional 
Water Board will need to issue a separate Order, to consider the use of recycled 
water for groundwater recharge.
Although the discharge poin t to Cooper's Creek overlies the Beaumont 
Groundwater Management Zone (GMZ), studies have shown that very little 
wastewater actually recharges that GMZ and that the discharge actually 
recharges the San Timoteo GMZ.

===PLANNED CHANGES===

Planned Changes
Although the Discharger has built the recycled water desalinization facility to comply 
with TDS and TIN effluent limitations in the sorthest time possible, the Discharger has 
experienced difficulties in the start up of the membrane bioreactor (MBR) that fe eds 
the reverse osomosis (RO) system and has not been able to achieve the TDS and 
TIN removal efficiency needed to comply with the TDS and TIN effluent limitations of 
Order No. R8 -2015 -0026. The Discharger is in the process of solving the start up 
operatio nal issues caused by the premature failure of the microfiltration membranes of 
the MBR and is replacing the original membranes with membranes from a different 
and stablished manufacturer.   Also, once the microfiltration membranes are replaced 
the violations of effluent limitations for turbidity and total coliform will be addressed.
