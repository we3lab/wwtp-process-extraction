WASTEWATER SYSTEM OPERATION SUMMARY
Spreckels Wastewater Treatment Plant (Wastewater System) is a Class I wastewater
treatment facility . CA American Water must staff a Grade I chief plant operator for
proper operation of the Wastewater System. The current chief plant operator is Robert
Moss Grade 3, license number 41116.
In the notice of intent, CA American Water proposes that the Wastewater System is
designed to treat and dispose of 180,000 gallons per day to a secondary treatment
level.6 The Wastewater System consists of the treatment technologies shown in Table
2. The Central Coast Water Board is permitting the operation and disposal of the
system as described below. CA American Water must not change operation or disposal
technology without Central Coast Water Board notification and review.

Table 2. Treatment Technology and Original Design Capacity
Headworks/Preliminary Treatment The headworks consist of two lift stations (Lift Station 1
and Lift Station 3). The stations do not have any influent
screening. Manual maintenance is performed to remove large debris from the suction side of influent pumps.
Primary and
Secondary Treatment The system consists of a 12-acre facultative pond
equipped with floating aerators. The pond depth is
estimated to be 10 feet and has a capacity of 30 million
gallons .
Wastewater Disposal
Method The facultative pond is not lined, and also serves as a
disposal pond.
Biosolids/Sludge Production and Handling Solids accumulated in the treatment pond a re removed by
dredging and disposed of at a nearby municipal landfill
facility as needed. N o sludge removal has occurred since
CA American Water has owned the facility.
The ultimate destination of biosolids produced is Monterey
Regional Waste Management District - Landfill Facility
located at 14201 Del Monte Boulevard in Marina
California .
