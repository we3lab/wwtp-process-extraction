The beneficial uses of underlying ground water are domestic, municipal, industrial, and agricultural
supply. The beneficial uses of surface water in the Yolo Bypass are agricultural supply for irrigation and
stock watering, contact and non-contact recreation, warm freshwater habitat, fish migration and spawning,
wildlife habitat and groundwater recharge.
Surface water drainage from the site is to Lamb Valley Slough, tributary to South Fork Willow Slough,
tributary to the Yolo Bypass.
