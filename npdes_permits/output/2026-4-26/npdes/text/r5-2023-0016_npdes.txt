The Facility discharges treated wastewater at three separate discharge points: the
Sacramento River (surface water discharge), a leachfield on property owned by the USFS
(land disposal), and to the Siskiyou Lake Golf Resort, Inc. for irrigation (recycled water).
Surface water discharge during the summer period (15 June through 14 September ) is
prohibited.
