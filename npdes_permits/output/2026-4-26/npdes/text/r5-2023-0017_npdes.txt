FACILITY DESCRIPTION
The Discharger provides sewerage service for the community of Redding and serves a
population of approximately 29,000. The design daily average flow capacity of the Facility
is 3.4 MGD .
A. Description of Wastewater and Biosolids Treatment and Controls
The treatment system at the Facility consists of bar screening; primary
sedimentation; alkalinity adjustment; biological treatment by activated sludge,
including nitrification and denitrification; secondary sedimentation; filtration;
disinfection; and dechlorination. Sludge is aerobically digested and dewatered using
a belt filter press. Dried biosolids are stored onsite at the location(s) identified in
Attachment C and are hauled to a landfill for disposal. The facility produces
approximately 100 dry metric tons of dried biosolids annually. Transportation and
disposal/reuse of the biosolids is regulated by U.S. EPA under 40 C.F.R. part 503.

ATTACHMENT F – FACT SHEET F-7D. Compliance Summary
During the previous permit term, the Discharger was subject to the following
enforcement actions:
•Administrative Civil Liability R5-2019-0527 was issued for violations occurring
between 29 August 2017 and 15 January 2019. The Discharger was fined
$12,000 in mandatory minimum penalties (MMPs) for total coliform and total
residual chlorine effluent limit violations. The Discharger waived their right to a
hearing and paid the amount in full.
•A Notice of Violation was issued on 5 August 2019 for violations occurring
between 1 November 2018 and 15 January 2019. The Discharger was assessed
$9,000 in MMPs for violations of total coliform and total chlorine residual effluent
limitations.
•A Notice of Violation was issued on 25 August 2021 for violations occurring
between 5 June 2019 and 23 February 2021. The Discharger was assessed
$0 in MMPs for violations of total coliform effluent limitations and deficient
monitoring violations.
•A Notice of Violation was issued on 28 July 2022 for violations occurring between
30 June 2021 and 7 August 2021. The Discharger was assessed $0 in MMPs for
violations of total coliform effluent limitations and deficient monitoring violations.
The compliance issues discussed above have been resolved and are not ongoing; 
there are no associated issues remaining at the Facility.

===PLANNED CHANGES===

Planned Changes
The Discharger is evaluating plans to add facultative sludge lagoons and sludge
drying beds to the Facility. The facultative sludge lagoons would be used in
conjunction with the aerobic digesters to increase the Facilit y’s ability to reduce
solids. The sludge drying beds would be used as the Facilit y’s primary method of
dewatering solids, however, the Discharger would maintain the belt filter press as a
backup for dewatering . If these treatment components are installed at the Facility,
the new sewage sludge treatment train process will be secondary clarifiers, aerobic
digesters, facultative sludge lagoons, and sludge drying beds.
