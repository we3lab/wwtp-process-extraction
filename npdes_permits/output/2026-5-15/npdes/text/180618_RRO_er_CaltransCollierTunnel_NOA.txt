C. Septic Tank
The domestic wastewater system serving the Facility includes a 6,000-gallon septic tank
that provides primary treatment of the wastewater. Septic tanks require proper operation,
maintenance, and monitoring in order to provide adequate and reliable wastewater
treatment. This includes periodic inspection and removal, as needed, of accumulated
solids. Requirements for septic tanks are included in General Order Section B.2, Septic
Systems, and in the enclosed MRP, at section titled “Septic Tank Monitoring and
Maintenance.” Operational maintenance and monitoring work on the septic system shall
be conducted by qualified service providers approved by the Regional Water Board
Executive Officer with appropriate oversight by the Discharger. The Discharger is
responsible for ensuring proper operation, maintenance, and monitoring of all septic tanks.
D. Subsurface Disposal System Requirements
The domestic wastewater system includes dispersal of treated effluent to subsurface soil
via a 5,200-square foot subsurface drip disposal system. The Discharger is required to
include a constructed primary disposal system with capacity for 100 percent of design
flows, and a 100 percent capacity designated reserve area. The reserve area is for future
use, in case the existing disposal system is found to be inadequate. The reserve area is
required to remain available until needed. The Discharger has designated a 100 percent
capacity reserve disposal area.
