Monterey Dunes Colony Association’s wastewater system is located between 0.6 and
1.2 miles north of the mouth of the Salinas River. Depth between leachfields and first-
encountered groundwater is approximately ten feet. The discharge area is located on
rolling sand dunes consisting of sandy soils to a depth of 14 feet.
