The current treatment system includes:

• Denitrification in Cell C of Pond 2;
• Sludge storage in Cells A and B of Pond 2;
• Coagulant addition;
• Pressure sand filtration;
• Ultraviolet light disinfection;
• Chlorine contact chamber;
