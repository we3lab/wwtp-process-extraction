9. Untreated wastewater flows from the influent pump station to the headworks for
preliminary treatment. Wastewater from the preliminary treatment system gravity
flows to the MBR system for secondary treatment. Secondary treated wastewater
then flows to the membrane filtration basins for further treatment and then to the
UV system for disinfection. The disinfected tertiary recycled water is then
discharged into three recharge basins.
10. Waste Activated Sludge (WAS) and foam from the MBR system are discharged
to sludge storage tanks. The tanks are aerated by a blower system to keep the
solids mixed and prevent them for turning anaerobic. Solids from the storage
tanks are pumped to screw presses for dewatering. Dewatered solids are
conveyed to a truck for hauling and disposal offsite. Dewatering liquor and other
plant discharges are collected in a process drain pump station and pumped to
the head of the plant upstream of the screens.
11. Chemical storage and dosing facilities are provided for sodium hypochlorite, citric
acid, dewatering polymer and other chemicals that may be required by the MBR
membrane manufacturer.
