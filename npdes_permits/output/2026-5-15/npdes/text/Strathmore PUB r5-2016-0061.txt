BACKGROUND
The Strathmore Public Utility District (PUD or District) owns and operates a wastewater treatment
facility (WWTF) that produces undisinfected secondary treated wastewater. The WWTF consists of a
clarigester, two oxidation ponds, six evaporation/percolation ponds, and two unlined sludge drying
beds. The existing WWTF was constructed in 1950 and has likely met its end of useful life. The
discharge from the WWTF is regulated by Waste Discharge Requirements Order 85-024.
In 2002 the Central Valley Water Board issued Cleanup and abatement Order R5-2002-0730 (CAO)
after the Discharger reported that pond and clarigester sludge contained toxic concentrations of
copper. The CAO required the Discharge to cleanup and dispose of copper laden sludge and land
application areas, cleanup dispose of and abate conditions of impacted soils, and characterize the
horizontal and vertical extent of groundwater degradation and pollution, if any, caused by the handling
of copper laden sludge.
