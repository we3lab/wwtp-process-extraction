The tertiary treatment system at the Facility consists of the following:
· Bar screening
· Two lined equalization basins
· Primary sedimentation
· Activated sludge (including nitrification and denitrification)
· Secondary clarification
· Tertiary filtration
· Ultraviolet light (UV) disinfection
· Cascade aerator
Biosolids are treated by an anaerobic digester, solar dried before further dewatering
using a belt filter press, and reused through land application by Synagro West, LLC.
