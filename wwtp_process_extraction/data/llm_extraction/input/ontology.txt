PROCESSES:
Process | A process applied to water treatment.
PhysicalProcess | A unit process that relies on physical forces to separate or treat water.
Separation | The most generic process for separation of any two constituents. | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
Elutriation | A process that uses a stream of fluid flowing upward to separate a mixture of particles based on their size, shape, and density. | SubProcessOf: Separation
Screening | A unit process that removes large solids from water, such as leaves, branches, and other debris. | SubProcessOf: Separation
Sedimentation | A unit process that allows solids to settle out of water by gravity. | SubProcessOf: Separation
Stripping | Gas stripping for removal of dissolved gases such as ammonia and volatile organic compounds. | SubProcessOf: Separation
Filtration | A unit process that removes smaller particles from water by passing it through a filter. | SubProcessOf: Separation
MediaFiltration | A filtration process that uses a bed of media (as opposed to a membrane-based process). | SubProcessOf: Filtration
SlowSandFiltration | A filtration process that uses a bed of sand to remove impurities at low flow rates. | SubProcessOf: MediaFiltration
RapidSandFiltration | A filtration process that uses a bed of sand to remove impurities at high flow rates. | SubProcessOf: MediaFiltration
Flotation | A unit process that uses buoyancy to separate solids from water, often using air bubbles. | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
GasTransfer | A unit process that transfers gases, such as oxygen or carbon dioxide, into or out of water. | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
Aeration | A unit process that aerates (i.e., transfers oxygen into water). | SubProcessOf: GasTransfer
Mixing | A unit process that mixes water with chemicals or other substances to achieve a desired effect. | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
Recirculation | A process that returns a portion of a treated or intermediate flow back upstream to maintain hydraulic, solids, or biological performance. | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
Thickening | A process to increase the solids content of a sludge or slurry. | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
Dewatering | A process to remove or separate water from another material, typically solids. | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
Drying | Drying of biosolids to reduce moisture content. | SubProcessOf: Dewatering, Evaporation
Centrifugation | A process that uses centrifugal force to separate substances of different densities. | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
Solidification | A process of turning a liquid into a solid (e.g., freezing). | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
Crystallization | A process of forming solid crystals from a solution. | SubProcessOf: Solidification
Evaporation | A process of turning a liquid into a vapor. | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
Condensation | A process of turning a vapor into a liquid. | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
Adsorption | A process by which substances bind to a surface. | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
GranularActivatedCarbon | A process that uses an activated carbon filter to adsorb impurities from water. | SubProcessOf: Adsorption, Filtration
MembraneProcess | A process that uses a semi-permeable membrane to separate substances. | SubProcessOf: Filtration
ReverseOsmosis | A core membrane separation process for desalination and advanced purification. | SubProcessOf: MembraneProcess
ClosedCircuitReverseOsmosis | A specific RO configuration explored for optimization, capable of adapting to unmeasured changes like feed salinity. | SubProcessOf: ReverseOsmosis
OsmoticallyAssistedReverseOsmosis | A process with validated Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) models for cost optimization. | SubProcessOf: ReverseOsmosis
FeedReversalReverseOsmosis | A dynamic and cyclic design to mitigate scaling and balance salt/foulant load, enabling high recovery. | SubProcessOf: ReverseOsmosis
MembraneDistillation | A thermal membrane process, often subject to CFD modeling for optimization of heat and mass transfer. | SubProcessOf: MembraneProcess
Electrodialysis | A process that uses ion-exchange membranes and an electric potential to separate ions from water. | SubProcessOf: ChemicalProcess, PhysicalProcess
ElectroDialyticCrystallization | A novel integrated process combining electrodialysis and crystallization for energy-efficient Zero-Liquid Discharge (ZLD) by maintaining a saturated brine stream. | SubProcessOf: Electrodialysis, Crystallization
Comminution | Process of reducing solid materials into smaller particles through mechanical forces such as crushing, grinding, impact, shear, or compression. | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
BiosolidsDisposal | Generic process for unspecified disposal of biosolids (e.g., incineration, land application, landfilling, etc.). | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
Composting | Biological degradation of organic materials under controlled aerobic conditions. | SubProcessOf: BiosolidsDisposal
LandApplication | Beneficial reuse of biosolids by spreading on agricultural land. | SubProcessOf: BiosolidsDisposal
Landfill | Disposal of biosolids in a landfill. | SubProcessOf: BiosolidsDisposal
ChemicalProcess | A unit process that involves the addition of chemicals to water to achieve a desired effect.
ChemicalAddition | Addition of chemicals, such as coagulant, polymer, disinfectant, etc. | SubProcessOf: ChemicalProcess
Coagulation | A unit process that adds chemicals to water to cause small particles to clump together. | SubProcessOf: ChemicalAddition
Electrocoagulation | An electrified pretreatment technology using sacrificial anodes to release coagulant precursors for contaminant removal. | SubProcessOf: Coagulation, PhysicalProcess
Flocculation | A unit process that gently mixes water to allow coagulated particles to form larger clumps. | SubProcessOf: ChemicalProcess, Mixing
UVDisinfection | A disinfection process that uses ultraviolet light to inactivate microorganisms. | SubProcessOf: Disinfection
Disinfection | A unit process that uses chemicals or other methods to kill or inactivate harmful microorganisms in water. | SubProcessOf: ChemicalProcess
Chlorination | A disinfection process that uses chlorine or chlorine compounds. | SubProcessOf: Disinfection
Dechlorination | A post-disinfection process that removes excess chlorine compounds. | SubProcessOf: Disinfection
ThermalDisinfection | Heat-based pathogen inactivation such as pasteurization. | SubProcessOf: Disinfection
pHAdjustment | A unit process that adjusts the pH of water to a desired level using chemicals. | SubProcessOf: ChemicalAddition
Neutralization | pH adjustment to neutral, typically with a base, used in processes like high-density sludge treatment. | SubProcessOf: pHAdjustment
Oxidation | A unit process that adds oxidizing agents to water to remove contaminants or improve water quality. | SubProcessOf: ChemicalProcess
AdvancedOxidation | Processes combining UV light with oxidants (e.g., UV-AOP) to destroy contaminants. | SubProcessOf: Oxidation
Electrooxidation | An advanced oxidation process often combined with EC to enhance contaminant removal and inactivation, particularly for viruses. | SubProcessOf: Oxidation, PhysicalProcess
Ozonation | A water treatment process that uses ozone as an oxidant. | SubProcessOf: Oxidation
Softening | Chemical precipitation of calcium/magnesium | SubProcessOf: ChemicalAddition
ChemicalPrecipitation | Removal of nutrients or metals using coagulants | SubProcessOf: ChemicalProcess
Reduction | Mechanism by which contaminants are transformed through the gain of electrons. | SubProcessOf: ChemicalProcess
SolventExtraction | A non-membrane, non-thermal method using a solvent to selectively extract water from highly saline brines. | SubProcessOf: ChemicalProcess
HighDensitySludge | A method for treating acid mine drainage involving neutralization. | SubProcessOf: ChemicalProcess, PhysicalProcess
IonExchange | A process in which ions are exchanged between a solution and an ion-exchange resin or membrane. | SubProcessOf: ChemicalProcess, PhysicalProcess
Electrolysis | A process that uses a direct electric current to drive an otherwise non-spontaneous chemical reaction. | SubProcessOf: ChemicalProcess
Combustion | A process that combusts biosolids or biogas. | SubProcessOf: ChemicalProcess, PhysicalProcess
FluidizedBedIncineration | A process that combusts a constantly moving bed of biosolids. | SubProcessOf: Combustion
MultipleHearthIncineration | A process that combusts biosolids as they move through a series of stacked hearths with controlled air flow. | SubProcessOf: Combustion
Cogeneration | A process that combusts biosolids or biogas to produce electricity and heat simultaneously. | SubProcessOf: Combustion
BiologicalProcess | A unit process that utilizes biological activity for water treatment.
Nitrification | Process by which ammonia is oxidized to nitrite and subsequently nitrate. | SubProcessOf: BiologicalProcess
Denitrification | Process by which nitrate is deoxidized to nitrogen gas and nitrogen oxides. | SubProcessOf: BiologicalProcess
FiveStageBardenpho | A five-stage biological nutrient removal process specifically designed for nitrogen and phosphorus. | SubProcessOf: ActivatedSludge, Nitrification, Denitrification
FourStageBardenpho | A four-stage biological nutrient removal process with two anoxic and two aerobic zones. | SubProcessOf: ActivatedSludge, Nitrification, Denitrification
ActivatedSludge | Aerobic suspended-growth biological treatment | SubProcessOf: Separation, BiologicalProcess
AO | Two-stage process with anoxic zone followed by aerobic zone for biological nitrogen removal. | SubProcessOf: ActivatedSludge, Nitrification, Denitrification
MLE | Modification of AO a with internal recycle. | SubProcessOf: AO
A2O | Anaerobic-anoxic-aerobic process for simultaneous biological nitrogen and phosphorus removal. | SubProcessOf: ActivatedSludge, Nitrification, Denitrification
UCT | Variant of A2O with modified recycle streams for phosphorus removal. | SubProcessOf: A2O
Biofiltration | A filtration method that uses biological processes to remove contaminants. | SubProcessOf: BiologicalProcess, Filtration
TricklingFiltration | Trickling filter using any media (e.g., plastic, sand, gravel, etc.) | SubProcessOf: Biofiltration
BiologicallyActiveFiltration | A biologically activated filtration column. | SubProcessOf: Biofiltration
Microfiltration | 0.1 to 1 micron-scale filtration | SubProcessOf: Filtration
Ultrafiltration | 0.01 to 0.1 micron-scale filtration | SubProcessOf: Filtration
Backwashing | A cleaning process in which water, and sometimes air, is driven backward through a filter or membrane system to remove accumulated solids. | SubProcessOf: Cleaning
AirScouring | A cleaning process that uses injected air to dislodge accumulated foulants or solids from filter media or membrane surfaces. | SubProcessOf: Cleaning
Purging | A process that flushes retained liquid, solids, or gas from equipment or piping to restore operating conditions or prepare for another cycle. | SubProcessOf: Cleaning
Digestion | A biological process that breaks down organic matter over a long duration in a tank. | SubProcessOf: BiologicalProcess
Hydrolysis | A process reaction in which a water molecule is consumed to split a larger molecule into smaller fragments. | SubProcessOf: ChemicalProcess
ThermalHydrolysis | A process that breaks down complex organic matter into soluble compounds using heat and pressure. | SubProcessOf: Hydrolysis
Cleaning | A maintenance-oriented process that removes accumulated solids, foulants, or residual materials from water treatment equipment. | SubProcessOf: PhysicalProcess
AnaerobicDigestion | A biological process that breaks down organic matter in the absence of oxygen. | SubProcessOf: Digestion
AerobicDigestion | A biological process that breaks down organic matter in the presence of oxygen. | SubProcessOf: Digestion
