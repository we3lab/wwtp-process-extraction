ProcessType | A process applied to water treatment.
PhysicalProcess | A unit process that relies on physical forces to separate or treat water.

Sedimentation | A unit process that allows solids to settle out of water by gravity. | SubProcess: Separation
Stripping | Gas stripping for removal of dissolved gases such as ammonia and volatile organic compounds. | SubProcess: Separation
Filtration | A unit process that removes smaller particles from water by passing it through a filter. | SubProcess: Separation
MediaFiltration | A filtration process that uses a bed of media (as opposed to a membrane-based process). | SubProcess: Filtration
SlowSandFiltration | A filtration process that uses a bed of sand to remove impurities at low flow rates. | SubProcess: MediaFiltration
RapidSandFiltration | A filtration process that uses a bed of sand to remove impurities at high flow rates. | SubProcess: MediaFiltration
Flotation | A unit process that uses buoyancy to separate solids from water, often using air bubbles. | SubProcess: PhysicalProcess
GasTransfer | A unit process that transfers gases, such as oxygen or carbon dioxide, into or out of water. | SubProcess: PhysicalProcess
Aeration | A unit process that aerates (i.e., transfers oxygen into water). | SubProcess: GasTransfer
Mixing | A unit process that mixes water with chemicals or other substances to achieve a desired effect. | SubProcess: PhysicalProcess
Recirculation | A process that returns a portion of a treated or intermediate flow back upstream to maintain hydraulic, solids, or biological performance. | SubProcess: PhysicalProcess
Dewatering | A process to remove or separate water from another material, typically solids. | SubProcess: PhysicalProcess
Drying | Drying of biosolids to reduce moisture content. | SubProcess: Dewatering, Evaporation
Centrifugation | A process that uses centrifugal force to separate substances of different densities. | SubProcess: PhysicalProcess

Crystallization | A process of forming solid crystals from a solution. | SubProcess: Solidification
