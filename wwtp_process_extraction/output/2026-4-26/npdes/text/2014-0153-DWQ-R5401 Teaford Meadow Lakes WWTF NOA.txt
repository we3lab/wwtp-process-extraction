DESCRIPTION OF DISCHARG E
The Facility is located in Madera County, southwest of Bass Lake, CA and South of
Oakhurst, CA off of Teaford Saddle Road (Road 223) (37.2692 °, -119.5727 °). The
Facility receives wastewater from approximately 66 households and treats the domestic
wastewater using an activated sludge treatment system . At the Facility, the domestic
wastewater gravity flows from the collection system through a manual bar screen into
the aeration basin. Solids collected from the manual bar screen are collected during
normal ope rations and deposited into a disposal container. Once full, the container is
disposed of at an approved landfill.
Wastewater is then treated in an extended aeration basin and the aeration blower is
stopped to allow sludge to settle at the bottom. A contractor pulls sludge from the
aeration basin once per quarter. This sludge is taken to the nearby Oakhurst
Wastewater Treatment Plant for processing, dewatering, and disposal. The secondary
treatment occurs at the aeration basin with a positive displacement blower that supplies
air to diffusers in the aeration basin. After this , the wastewater is sent through a clarifier
and then disinfected with 12.5% sodium hypochlorite in a contact tank.
From the contact tank , the treated wastewater is sent to two spray field zones , Spray
Field Zones 1 and 2. Spray Field Zone 1 is approximately 100 feet up hill from Little
Fine Gold Creek (37.2695 °, -119.5733 °). Spray Field Zone 2 is east of Little Fine Gold
Creek and the WWTF (37.2691 °, -119.5722 °). There is an unlined emergency pond
connected at the Facility that is primarily used to capture any runoff from the spray
fields. The average effluent flow from the WWTF is approximately 3,600 gallons per day
(gpd).
