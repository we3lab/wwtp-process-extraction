No. 1 Treatment train no. 1 is a Swanson advanced integrated
wastewater pond system (AIWPS) that consists of one primary facultative pond (Pond 2, volume = 5.2 million gallons) and one secondary aerobic pond (Pond 4, volume = 2.2 million gallons). Pond 4 can recirculate about 25 percent of the daily inflow back
to Pond 2 in the AIWPS system during daylight hours. A 5HP recirculation pump operating at 415 gallons per minute achieves this design parameter. Pond 2 must be equipped with 50 HP of surface aeration (two at 15HP and one at 20 HP) and Pond 4
must be equipped with 10 HP of surface aeration ( one at 10HP).
The AIWPS is designed to produce effluent concentration of 5 -
day biochemical oxygen demand (BOD) at 25 milligrams per liter
(mg/L) and total suspended solids at 100 mg/L, when operating
in stabilized mode. Pond aeration is required to maintain a dissolved oxygen level at about 2.0 mg/L to suppress odors. This treatment train produces a much higher quality of effluent than a typical primary wastewater treatment sy stem and is
considered secondary treatment but it is not designed to
produce a level of secondary treated wastewater as defined in the Code of Federal Regulations title 40, chapter I, subchapter
D, part 133 section 133.1028.
Secondary Treatment Train No. 2 Treatment train no. 2 consists of a Parkson Biolac ® Wave -Ox
activated sludge with extended aeration system and two integral clarifiers. Alternating anoxic and oxic zones within the bubbled basin are created by controlling the fine bubbles emanating from the floating aeration chains, therefore achieving nit rification and
denitrication. Airlift pumps collect and return activated sludge (a.k.a. RAS) to the front of the aeration basin. The Biolac® is
designed to produce an effluent concentration of 5-day BOD at
10 mg/L, total suspended solids at 12 mg/L, ammoni a as
nitrogen at 1 mg/L, total nitrogen removal of 80- 90% and less
than 8 mg/L, and total phosphorus at 2 mg/L. This Wastewater
System treatment train no. 2 is designed to treat up to 0.96 MGD
