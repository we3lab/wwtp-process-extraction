DISCHARGE DESCRIPTION
The Facility treats domestic wastewater produced from approximately 104 wastewater
connections from Millerton Lake Mobile Home Village (shown in Attachment A) . WDR
No. 85-255 specifies a 30- day average daily dry weather flow limitation of 22,500
gallons per day (gpd) . A second phase to expand the mobile home park and
wastewater treatment facility (to a total design capacity of 50,000 gpd) was originally
planned but was never built.
Attachment C is a process flow diagram for the Facility. The Facility is an extended
aeration activated sludge package plant. The Facility includes a chlorine contact
chamber ; however, chlorine is not currently used in the contact chamber since WDRs
Order 85- 255 did not require the effluent to be disinfected . The effluent is discharged to
an unlined percolation/evaporation pond.
Since the original construction (1980s ), the effluent percolation/evaporation pond was
divided into two ponds by a dirt berm (which function now in series) and an external
anaerobic sludge digester was added . Approximately 1,500 gallons of sludge is
pumped from the digester by an approved hauler every 21 days or sooner as needed.
