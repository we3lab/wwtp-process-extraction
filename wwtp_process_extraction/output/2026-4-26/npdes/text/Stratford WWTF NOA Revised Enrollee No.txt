DISCHARGE DESCRIPTION
WDRs Order 82-068 regulates the discharge of the District’s WWTF and allows a discharge of
up to 0.15 million gallons per day (mgd) to 20 acres of evaporation/percolation ponds.

The WWTF is at the southeast corner of Empire Street and 5th Avenue in Section 17, Townshi p
20 South, Range 20 East, Mount Diablo Base & Meridian in Kings County. The proposed
upgrades to the WWTF include a new headworks, four facultative ponds, two storage ponds ,
and five evaporation/percolation ponds . Based on data from 2015 through 2017, flows at the
WWTF average about 60,000 gpd, and range from 10,000 gpd (minimum) to 123,000 gpd
(maximum).
