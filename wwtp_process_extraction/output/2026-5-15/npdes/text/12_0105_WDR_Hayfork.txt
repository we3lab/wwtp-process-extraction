B. General Facility Information
The Discharger owns and operates a wastewater collection, treatment, and disposal
facility that provides sewerage service to the community of Hayfork. The Hayfork
community currently supports a population of about 2,300 people. The Facility has a
septage dump station located near the headworks, and accepts sewage trucked from
within Trinity County.
C. Existing Wastewater Treatment and Reclamation Facility
The existing Facility is designed to provide secondary treatment for an average dry
weather flow of 0.243 mgd, and peak wet weather flow at 0.729 mgd. Current average dry
weather flows at the facility are approximately 0.065 mgd. The existing Facility consists of
a collection system, an influent bar screen and headworks with influent flow meter, two
aerated primary treatment ponds, a secondary treatment pond, an effluent holding pond,
two wetland polishing ponds and four percolation ponds for final disposal. A chlorination
disinfection system was submitted as part of the final facility design, but was never
constructed. The existing Facility also includes 25 acres of vacant land owned by the
Discharger that is irrigated with undisinfected secondary effluent in violation of Order No.
