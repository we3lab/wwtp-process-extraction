2. Design Characteristics
Currently onsite are one 7.2 mgd conventional tertiary sand filter (DynaSand), two 5.5
mgd conventional tertiary cloth filters (AquaDisk), 10 percolation ponds which vary in
size, five banks of UV disinfection, 18 containment wells and 13 relief wells. On-site
extraction wells are located within and down-gradient of the percolation basins.
Groundwater monitoring wells are located within the same area. The 7.2 mgd
conventional tertiary sand filter was constructed and placed into service in 2000 to
augment treatment capacity during wet weather conditions. The two 5.5 mgd
conventional tertiary cloth filters were placed in service in 2008 for the same purpose.
