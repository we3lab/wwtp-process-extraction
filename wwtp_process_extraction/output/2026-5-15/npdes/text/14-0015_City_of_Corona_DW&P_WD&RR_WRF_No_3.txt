A. Wastewater and Biosolids Treatment or Controls
WRF-3 is an activated sludge plant with microfiltration. The treatment system includes
two wet wells with two drum screens for preliminary treatment. Following screening the
wastewater flows to an activated sludge tank that consists of 3 chambers with
microfiltration. The wastewater is aerated and filtered in the same tanks and waste
sludge is pumped to the sewer system to be treated at the City's WRF Nos. 1 and 2.
Following the activated sludge/microfilters the wastewater flows to a chlorine contact
tank. When there is a discharge to the creek, sodium bisulfite is used for dechlorination.
Stormwater runoff from the facility is collected in an on site retention basin and pumped
to the headworks for treatment. No stormwater from the facility is discharged to
Temescal Creek.
