C. Planned Upgrades
The Permittee owns and operates a wastewater treatment and disposal facility and is in the design
phases of developing a new recycled water storage and irrigation system. This Order is
established in support of a Facility upgrade to rehabilitate the headworks with new influent pumps;
convert the sand filter into a treatment lagoon (No. 3); dredge lagoon No. 1 and line lagoons 1 and 3
with synthetic liners; construct two irrigation storage ponds with an irrigation pump station; and
develop a recycled water irrigation field. In addition, depending on the availability of funding, the
Facility upgrades may include additional rehabilitation of the headworks to include fine screening.
