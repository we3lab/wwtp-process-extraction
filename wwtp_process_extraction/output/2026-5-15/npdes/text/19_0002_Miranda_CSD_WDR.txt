The Discharger owns and operates the wastewater treatment and disposal system
serving 115 residential and commercial connections. The system is comprised of
individual septic tanks, a small-diameter gravity collection system, a mixing
chamber, a recirculation tank, two gravel filter beds, a chlorination system and a
percolation pond. Individual septic tank solids are hauled off-site for disposal at a
permitted disposal facility via a Humboldt County permitted septage pumper truck.
The percolation pond is located approximately 75 feet from the current east bank of
the South Fork Eel River.
