DESCRIPTION OF DISCHARGE
The Facility is located at 36550 Road 44 in Kingsburg, in Tulare County, and is about
8.5 miles southeast of the City of Kingsburg (Latitude 36.4542°, Longitude -119.4729°).
The Facility is owned by Tulare County Resource Management Agency and is operated
by Central Cal Waterworks, Inc. The Facility serves the domestic wastewater treatment
needs of the Traver service area, which has a population of about 700, according to a
2020 census, and receives domestic wastewater from approximately 202 connections.
The Facility features a dual facultative lagoon system (operated in parallel), and two
disposal ponds. Wastewater from the service area collection system is directed to a lift
station prior to entering the Facility headworks, which consists of a flow meter, grinder,
and bar screen. After the headworks, a splitter box directs the comminuted wastewater
to one of the two facultative lagoons, each of which are equipped with a
five-horsepower aerator. Following aeration in the lagoons, wastewater is directed to
one of two disposal ponds via another splitter box that also has the capability of sending
the wastewater back to the headworks for further treatment. The previously permitted
30-day average daily dry weather flow for the WWTF (specified in WDRs Order No. 88-
098) is 89,000 gallons per day (gpd).
FACILITY SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS
