A. Description of Wastewater and Biosolids Treatment and Controls
Secondary treatment at the Facility is accomplished with screening and aerated grit removal,
primary sedimentation using clarifiers, flow equalization, and activated sludge treatment
followed by secondary clarification. Secondary-treated effluent is either discharged to the
Pacific Ocean through the San Elijo Ocean Outfall (SEOO), or receives tertiary treatment for
reuse applications in the San Dieguito Water District (SDWD), Santa Fe Irrigation District
(SFID), Olivenhain Municipal Water District (OMWD), and City of Del Mar.
Screenings from the headworks and solids from grit removal are collected onsite and trucked
to a local landfill. Sludge treatment consists of dissolved air flotation thickening, anaerobic

The 3.02-MGD tertiary treatment portion of the Facility (the Advanced Water Purification
Facility, AWPF) is designed to comply with the State Water Board Division of Drinking Water
(DDW) criteria for “disinfected tertiary recycled water”. The AWPF consists of two parallel
treatment trains. Train No. 1 provides coagulation, flocculation, and granular media filtration.
Train No. 2 provides both microfiltration (MF) and reverse osmosis (RO). The San Diego
Water Board issued written notice to the Discharger on March 15, 2013 authorizing the
initiation of discharge operations from the MF and RO facility. Spent washwater from the MF
units is stored in a sump and directed to the Facility primary clarifiers for treatment. RO
treatment is provided by units featuring thin film composite (TFC) membranes that achieve
approximately a 75 percent recovery rate. In its ROWD, the Discharger indicated that one-half
of the RO production capacity addressed within Order No. R9-2000-10 has been installed,
