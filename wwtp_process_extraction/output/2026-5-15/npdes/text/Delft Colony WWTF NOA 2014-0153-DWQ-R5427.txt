The coordinates for the pond system at the WWTF are latitude 36.508°,
longitude -119.448°. The RWD indicates wastewater enters the WWTF through a
gravity sewer main to the influent lift station. Wastewater is pumped from the lift station
consisting of a primary and standby pump, to the headworks through a 6-inch force
main. Influent flow is measured by a magnetic flowmeter at the entrance to the
headworks which includes a bar screen and grinder. The screened effluent is
discharged through a splitter box into one of the two facultative lagoons which contain
an aerator. The facultative lagoons typically operate in parallel, but the splitter box
design enables them to operate in series as needed. The lagoons discharge to a splitter
box enabling the return of treated effluent to the lagoons, recirculation via the lift station,
or discharge to the disposal ponds. The treated effluent is discharged into one of two
disposal ponds for percolation or evaporation. Typically, only one disposal pond is in
use while the other serves as a standby disposal pond.
The previously permitted 30-day average daily dry weather flow for the WWTF
(specified in WDRs Order 88-097) is 572,000 gallons per day (gpd); however, the RWD
indicates that the limit of 572,000 gpd was made in error as the WWTF has a design
capacity of only 57,200 gpd.
