1. Facility and Discharge Description. The Facility is located in Campo, an
unincorporated community located in southeastern San Diego County, within the
Campo Hydrologic Area (911.8) of the Tijuana Hydrologic Unit. The General
Order and this NOA regulate the discharge of waste from small wastewater
treatment systems and prescribe monitoring requirements for the treatment and
discharge of domestic wastewater. The Facility OWTS consists of a grease
interceptor, Imhoff tank, dosing tank, trickling filter, two secondary clarifiers,
solids contact tank, anoxic selector tank, effluent tank, eight percolations beds,
and two sludge drying beds. The maximum ten-year average flowrate for the
Facility is approximately 76,000 gallons per day (gpd). The effluent is circulated
to percolations beds located 0.5 mile from the Facility. The groundwater quality
monitoring network consists of five groundwater monitoring wells.
