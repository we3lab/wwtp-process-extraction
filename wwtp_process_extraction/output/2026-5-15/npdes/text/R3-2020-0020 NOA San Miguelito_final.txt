2. Wastewater System Operation Summary
San Miguelito Mutual Water Company wastewater treatment facility (Wastewater
System) is a Class I wastewater system. San Miguelito Mutual Water Company must
staff a Class I chief plant operator for proper operation of the Wastewater System. The
current chief plant operator is Ryan Smith, Grade II, License Number 28414.
The Wastewater System is capable of treating 150,000 gallons per day to a Secondary
treatment level. The Wastewater System consists of the treatment technologies and
treatment capabilities shown in Table 2.
