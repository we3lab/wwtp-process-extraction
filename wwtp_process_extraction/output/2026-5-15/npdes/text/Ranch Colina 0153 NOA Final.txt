2. WASTEWATER SYSTEM OPERATION SUMMARY
The treatment system consists of coarse screening, extended aeration, settling,
chlorination, and discharge to a holding pond. From the holding pond, wastewater is
disposed via spray irrigation on a hillside west of the treatment facility. Sludge is stored
onsite in a polyethylene tank. From the polyethylene tank, sludge will either be dried on
site in a concrete-lined drying bed and the dried biosolids taken to an authorized
biosolids disposal facility or pumped from the polyethylene tank as sludge and hauled
off site for disposal by a septic pumping service. A summary of the system is included in
Table 2 below. A facility site map and dispersal system layout are shown in Attachment
2.
