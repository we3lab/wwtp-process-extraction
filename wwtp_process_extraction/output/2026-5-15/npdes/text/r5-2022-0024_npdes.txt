A. Description of Wastewater and Biosolids Treatment and Controls
The Facility is a membrane bioreactor (MBR) system capable of treating up to
50,000 gallons per day (gpd). The treatment system consists of primary screening
using a rotating drum screen; a biological and tertiary treatment vessel (anaerobic
treatment chamber for denitrification, aerobic treatment chamber for nitrification and
membrane filter for microfiltration); a 9-channel UV disinfection system; an effluent
storage tank for backwashing the membrane filter; and a concrete wet well for
effluent storage prior to land discharge to the spray field. A lined, impervious
emergency storage basin with a capacity of approximately 300,000 gallons is
available for emergency storage.
Biosolids are collected in a 50-gallon plastic container and disposed off-site. No
biosolids are treated, stored, or discharged on site.
Disinfected tertiary treated wastewater is discharged to either land or to surface
water. When discharging to land (1 May – 30 September) the effluent is conveyed to
the concrete wet well where it is disposed to land (7.5-acre spray field with native
grasses and trees) via a sprinkler system. Discharge to the unnamed tributary of
Sanford Creek during wet weather months when land discharge is not possible
(1 October – 30 April).
