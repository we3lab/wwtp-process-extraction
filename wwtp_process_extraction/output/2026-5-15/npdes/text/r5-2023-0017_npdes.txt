E. Planned Changes
The Discharger is evaluating plans to add facultative sludge lagoons and sludge
drying beds to the Facility. The facultative sludge lagoons would be used in
conjunction with the aerobic digesters to increase the Facility’s ability to reduce
solids. The sludge drying beds would be used as the Facility’s primary method of
dewatering solids, however, the Discharger would maintain the belt filter press as a
backup for dewatering. If these treatment components are installed at the Facility,
the new sewage sludge treatment train process will be secondary clarifiers, aerobic
digesters, facultative sludge lagoons, and sludge drying beds.
