A. Facility and Discharge Description
The Microsoft Silicon Valley Campus is located on approximately 32.8 acres and consists of
existing and new structures. The structures include office workspace, hardware and
software labs, and amenities consisting of a conference center, retail store, fitness center,
and cafes. The site location is shown in Attachment A, which is attached and made part of
this Notice by reference.
Domestic wastewater from toilet flushing, sinks, showers, and the food preparation areas will
be collected and treated onsite to disinfected tertiary recycled water standards in
accordance with 22 CCR sections 60301.230 and 60301.320. The disinfected tertiary
treatment requirements are also referenced in section C.3 of this Notice. The tertiary treated
recycled water will be blended with collected and treated rainwater and used for flushed
fixtures (toilets and urinals) and irrigation. The estimated annual average domestic
wastewater flow, excluding rainwater, is 18,000 gallons per day (gpd), and the maximum
wastewater treatment system design capacity is approximately 25,000 gpd.
The wastewater will flow via a gravity collection system to the influent pump station. The
wastewater treatment system consists of the following components: two 10,000-gallon
primary treatment / septic tanks; a 20,000-gallon anoxic / equalization tank; a 10,000-gallon
aeration tank; Orenco’s AdvanTex AX-Max recirculating textile packed bed filters; 1,800
square feet of polishing vertical flow wetland (also indicated as vegetated recirculating
gravel filter system) with a recirculation tank; ultrafiltration membrane system consisting of
one skid with two Pentair X-Flow Aquaflex 55 hollow fiber membrane modules (pore size
0.03 microns); Aquionics In-line 400+ ultraviolet (UV) disinfection system; and ozone
disinfection primarily for color and odor removal. The AdvanTex system consists of four
Ax-Max 100 – 14 recirculation modules and four Ax-Max 100 - 14 filtrate modules. Effluent
from the packed bed filter system will be pumped to the 3,000-gallon recirculating tank
containing the duplex pumps that will dose the vegetated recirculating gravel filter system.
The Aquionics In-line 400+ UV disinfection system was conditionally accepted by the
Division of Drinking Water on October 15, 2008, with revisions to the acceptance conditions
noted in a letter dated February 19, 2015.
