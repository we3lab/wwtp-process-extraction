2.2. The Wastewater System is comprised of an advanced integrated
wastewater pond system (pond system) and can treat 600,000 gallons per day
to a secondary treatment level. Pond systems are typically comprised of
advanced facultative ponds with an anaerobic pit, digester pit, and aerated
zone (primary treatment), secondary facultative ponds with digester pits and
aerated zone (secondary treatment), secondary ponds backflow to primary
ponds, and maturation/settling ponds (polishing). In the advanced facultative
ponds (primary treatment), there is an anaerobic and aerobic zone. The
Meadowbrook WWTF uses two treatment trains (1 and 2) for primary and
secondary treatment. Flow is mixed following primary and secondary treatment
for final polishing in three maturation ponds in series. The Wastewater System
consists of the treatment technologies and treatment capabilities shown in
