B. Septic Tanks
The Facility includes three septic tanks that provide primary treatment of the
wastewater. The septic tanks require proper operation, maintenance, and monitoring in
order to provide adequate and reliable wastewater service. This includes periodic
inspection and removal, as needed, of accumulated solids. General Order Section B.2,
Septic Systems, and the enclosed MRP, at section III, Septic Tank Monitoring, include
requirements for the septic tanks. Qualified service providers may conduct operational
maintenance and monitoring on the septic system with appropriate oversight by the
Discharger. The Discharger is responsible for ensuring proper operation, maintenance,
and monitoring of the septic tanks.
C. Subsurface Disposal System Requirements
