19. California Code of Regulations, Title 27
California Code of Regulations, title 27, Division 2, (Title 27) specifies regulatory and
design criteria for discharges of solid wastes to land for treatment, storage, or disposal.
CCR, title 27, sections 20090(b) state that discharges are exempt from title 27
requirements for waste disposal provided the activity meets and continues to meet the
following pre-conditions:
"(b) Wastewater - Discharges of wastewater to land, including but not limited to
evaporation ponds, percolation ponds, or subsurface leachfields if the following
conditions are met:
(1) The applicable RWQCB has issued WDRs, reclamation requirements, or waived
such issuance;
(2) The discharge is in compliance with the applicable water quality control plan;
