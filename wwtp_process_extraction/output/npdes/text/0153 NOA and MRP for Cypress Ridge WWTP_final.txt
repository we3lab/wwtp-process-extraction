2. WASTEWATER SYSTEM OPERATION SUMMARY
In the early 2000s, the property experienced issues with recurrent effluent violations of
turbidity, total coliform, settleable solids, suspended solids, and biochemical oxygen
demand (BOD). Furthermore, the property experience issues with violations for multiple
incomplete reports. On January 24, 2008, The Central Coast Water Quality Control
Board issued a Cleanup and Abatement Order [CAO] (Order No. R3-2008-0022) to the
property owner. The property owner complied with the terms of the CAO and on May
19, 2011, the CAO was rescinded.
The treatment system is composed of conveyance piping, lift stations, a grinder, two
sequencing batch reactors, a decant tank, a sand filter, a chlorination system, a sludge
digestion and dewatering system, emergency storage pond, and a supervisory control
and data acquisition (SCADA) system. A summary of the system is included in Table 2
below. A facility site map and dispersal system layout are shown in Attachment 2.

Sand filtration is provided by a dual U.S Ftler, Zimpro Hydro-
Clear San Filter Model 714.
Effluent enters the chlorine contact chamber where a SCADA
controlled chlorine feed pump adds a 12.5% sodium
hypochlorite solution. A free chlorine analyzer (Hach 9184
SC) sends the chlorine level to the SCADA system for dosing
Disinfection rates. Then the water traverses through 2 PVC piping routes
(84 minute and 15 minute detention times). At this point, a
free chlorine analyzer (Hach CL17) reads the residual
chlorine and then the effluent continues through a third PVC
pipe (11 minute detention time).
