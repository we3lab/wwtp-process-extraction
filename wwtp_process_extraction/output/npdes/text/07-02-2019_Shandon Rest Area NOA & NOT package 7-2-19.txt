Shandon Safety Roadside Rest Area July 2, 2019
ATTACHMENT 1
OWNERSHIP, FACILITY, AND FACILITY OPERATION SUMMARY INFORMATION
• Shandon Safety Roadside Rest Area (Rest Area) is located on Highway 46
approximately 2.5 miles east of the city of Shandon, California.
• The California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) District 5, Inc. owns and
operates the Rest Area.
• Corby Kilmer is the District 5 Rest Area Coordinator.
• Dennis Glickman is the Caltrans District 5 North Region Manager.
• Jerry Marcotte is the Caltrans Senior Sanitary Engineer for this project.
• Bruce O’Hagan is the Caltrans Supervisor for Maintenance at the Rest Area.
• Lee Chaves is the Caltrans District 5 Maintenance Manager.
• Approximately 4,154 gallons per day of wastewater is currently generated at the Rest
Area and treated via onsite treatment and disposed systems.
Peak daily flow is 11,545 gallons per day.
o
Equalized flow to the treatment system is currently 4,585 gallons per day.
o
• The existing treatment system consists of:
A 25,000-gallon septic tank;
o
A 3,000 square foot gravel recirculation filter;
o
A 3,200 square foot subsurface wetland with wood chip media; and
o
A 600 feet of leach line.
o
• Changes to the treatment and disposal systems include:
Installation of a 25,000-gallon media/equalization tank split into 3 chambers;
o
 The first 2 chambers will contain plastic media; and
 The third chamber will equalize flows and dose the secondary treatments
system at a constant rate.
Changing the media in the subsurface wetland system from wood chips to a
o
combination of wood chips and drainage rock.
Increasing the leachfield area to accommodate 20-year future equalized average
o
daily flows of 5,731 gallons per day.
Changing the pump control system to a Cloud based SCADA system which will
o
provide flow data and pump failure alarms.
• Depth to groundwater is estimated to be 68 feet below ground surface.
