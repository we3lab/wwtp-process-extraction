Attachment C of the NOA is a process flow diagram, which provides a schematic
overview of the Facility’s wastewater system. The Facility receives domestic wastewater
from 66 homes. The Facility treats domestic wastewater through an activated sludge
treatment process. The wastewater is gravity fed to the Facility’s wet well. From the wet
well, the wastewater is screened by a manual bar, conveyed to the extended aeration
basin where solids are allowed to settle at the bottom of the basin. From the extended
aeration basin, wastewater is treated in a clarifier and then receives chlorine disinfection
in a contact tank. NOA 97-10-DWQ-R5163 mentions an emergency pond, but that
