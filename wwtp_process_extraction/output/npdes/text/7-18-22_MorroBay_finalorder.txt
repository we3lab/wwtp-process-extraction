2.1. Description of Wastewater and Biosolids Treatment and Controls
The Discharger owns and will operate the Facility, which will provide municipal and
industrial wastewater services to a population of approximately 10,600 people in
the City of Morro Bay. The Facility construction includes infrastructure, pipelines,
and appurtenances for influent, effluent, and recycled water.
2.1.1. Membrane Bioreactor (MBR) System
Raw wastewater will be conveyed to the Facility headworks, which will include two
coarse screen and grit removal trains, a SAFE system box, and two parallel fine
screens. Screenings and grit from the headworks facilities will be hauled offsite for
disposal. Screened effluent and return activated sludge will flow into two biological
nutrient removal (BNR) basins and then pumped to the MBR. The BNR-MBR
basins will be configured as two parallel activated sludge treatment trains, each
consisting of pre-anoxic, aerobic, and post-anoxic basins in-series.
Filtrate from the MBR will be stored in two RO feed tanks, which will provide water
for onsite uses and feed water to the RO system which produces water for IPR.
Overflows from these tanks will be discharged to an outfall balancing tank.
Tertiary treated effluent from the outfall balancing tank will be pumped through an
outfall disinfection chlorine contact pipeline and will then be dechlorinated by
sodium bisulfite injection prior to discharge through the ocean outfall.

2.1.3. Indirect Potable Reuse (IPR) System
Tertiary-treated effluent from the RO feed tanks may be sent to the outfall
balancing tank or the IPR system for advanced purification. IPR system treatment
consists of cartridge filters, an RO system, ultraviolet disinfection with advanced
oxidation (UV/AOP) chlorination, and a calcite contactor. The IPR system was
designed to accept a feed annual flow rate of 0.97 MGD and a peak hourly flow of
1.16 MGD. The RO recovery rate is expected to be between 80 and 85 percent,
such that for average influent flow rates of 0.97 MGD, the RO permeate would be
approximately 0.80 MGD, and the RO concentrate stream would be 0.17 MGD.
