1. FACILITY AND DISCHARGE DESCRIPTION
The Facility is located at 5702 Institution Road, San Bernardino in San Bernardino
County within the Bunker Hill B Groundwater Management Zone (groundwater
basin). The Facility’s OWTS collects domestic wastewater from a proposed 335,000
square foot warehouse building with office. The Facility’s OWTS includes one 5,000-
gallon equalization dosing tank, one moving bed bioreactor (MBBR) aerobic
wastewater treatment system (MicroSeptec Enviroserve CS Series ES25C), and two
5-foot diameter by 17-foot-deep seepage pits for final effluent disposal into the
subsurface of the site. The MicroSeptec CS Series ES25C system consists of a
7,500-gallon capacity tank that is divided into 5 compartments that include a primary
clarifier chamber, two aeration chambers, a final clarifier chamber, and an effluent
