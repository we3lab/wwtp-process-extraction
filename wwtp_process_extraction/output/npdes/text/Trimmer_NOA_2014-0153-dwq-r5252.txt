The Discharger shall comply with all septic system requirements specified in Section B.2 of the
General Order. The General Order states in Section B.2.c that to the maximum extent possible,
RV, portable toilet, or similar wastes shall not be discharged to a septic tank or functionally
equivalent system (e.g., Imhoff tank) without subsequent additional treatment (e.g., aerated
pond, recirculating sand filter, etc.) prior to disposal.

The General Order states in Section B.2.d that septic tanks shall be pumped when any of the
following conditions exists:
1. The combined thickness of sludge and scum exceeds one-third of the tank depth of the first
compartment.
2. The scum layer is within 3 inches of the outlet device.
3. The sludge layer is within 8 inches of the outlet device.
The Discharge shall comply with all pond system requirements specified in Section B.5 of the
General Order. Section B.5.d of the General Order states that objectionable odors shall not
create nuisance conditions beyond the limits of the facility, A dissolved oxygen concentration
less than 1.0 mg/L in the upper one foot of any wastewater pond shall be evidence of the
potential to generate objectionable odors.
