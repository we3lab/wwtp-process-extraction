GENERAL STORE, WASTE WATER TREATMENT FACILITY, MARIPOSA COUNTY
On 14 August 2010, you submitted a Report of Waste Discharge to obtain coverage under
the above referenced General Order. You own and operate a package wastewater
treatment facility (WWTF) that has three service connections: 1. the former Black Oak
General Store, 2. a vacant mobile home, and 3. a vacant church. You propose to replace
the vacant mobile home with an 1800 square-foot manufactured home, which will likely be
the sole contributor to the WWTF for the next three years. Discharge from the WWTF is to
a small evaporation/percolation pond. The RWD indicates the design capacity of the
WWTF and evaporation/percolation pond is 2,500 gpd.
The WWTF and evaporation/percolation pond are at 3460 Highway 132 in Mariposa County
in the Northwest ¼ of Section 3, Township 3 South, Range 15 East, Mount Diablo Base and
Meridian.
The WWTF and disposal area are in the Merced River Hydrologic Unit, Coulterville
Hydrologic Subarea (537.21). The Water Quality Control Plan for the Sacramento River
