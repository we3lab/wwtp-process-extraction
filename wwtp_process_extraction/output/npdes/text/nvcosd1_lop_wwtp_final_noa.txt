The Microfiltration Membrane Bioreactor (MBR) treatment system at the Facility
consists of the following:
 influent headworks with course screening followed by fine screening;
 biological nutrient removal processes where BOD reduction, nitrogen removal,
and phosphorus removal occurs;
 a series of permeate pumps that pull the wastestream through the membranes
by vacuum provides solids separation; and
 disinfection includes two parallel ultraviolet light (UV) systems.
The biological treatment system is configured in two identical parallel treatment
trains so that a single train can be taken off-line for maintenance during the dry
months of the year. Scum and waste activated sludge produced from waste material
in the MBR tanks is digested in aerobic sludge digesters followed by centrifuge
dewatering. The dewatered sludge is taken off-site for disposal in a landfill.
