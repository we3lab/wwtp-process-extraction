8. The WWTF consists of a collection system, two aerated ponds (Ponds 1 and 3), one
unaerated pond (Pond 2), chlorination facilities, a storage reservoir, and the LAAs. All of
the ponds and the reservoir are unlined. Little Indian Creek is located topographically
downgradient from the LAAs and an unnamed tributary passes through the LAAs.
Sludge is hauled off site to a permitted landfill. A current site plan is included in Attachment
B, which is attached hereto and made part of the Order by reference.
9. The wastewater is treated in Ponds 1 through 3. Design data for the existing ponds are
summarized below.

10. The storage reservoir is used for storage of treated and disinfected effluent. The reservoir
has a capacity of 185 acre-feet (60 MG) at two feet of freeboard. A diversion ditch around
the storage reservoir has been modified to divert stormwater runoff away from the storage
reservoir.
