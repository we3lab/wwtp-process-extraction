the Facility. The complaint alleged the Discharger violated the terms of its NPDES Permit
(San Diego Water Board Order No. 96-50/ NPDES Permit CA0108928) by failing to treat the
Facility effluent to secondary treatment standards and by violating other effluent limitations.
