The WWTF provides treatment and disposal service for domestic wastewater generated
by the Dixon Migrant Center, which provides housing for approximately 370 residents
during the harvest season. The Dixon Migrant Center consists of separate housing and
wastewater disposal areas. The housing area occupies approximately 27 acres
(Assessor’s Parcel No 0143-060-070), and the wastewater ponds occupy approximately
5 acres of a 33-acre parcel (Assessor’s Parcel No 0143-060-110). The housing area is
in the northwest quarter of the northeast quarter, and the wastewater ponds are in the
eastern half of the southeast quarter of Section 8, T6N, R2E, MDB&M. A site location
map is shown on Attachment A, which is attached hereto and is made part of this NOA
by reference.
