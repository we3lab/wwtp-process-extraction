7. The Discharger conveys excess wastewater approximately 9000 feet from the Lansing
Avenue WWTF to the TRC WWTF through a 12-inch force main. The TRC WWTF consists
of: headworks, including an inline comminutor and a magnetic flow meter with bypass
provided in a below ground precast concrete vault, two aeration ponds, two polishing ponds,
and four evaporation/percolation ponds, shown in Attachment B, herein made part of this
Order by reference.
8. Overflow weirs with adjustable telescoping valves between the two aeration ponds and two
clarifier ponds to allow the depth of wastewater to be adjusted from four to six feet. The
maximum operating depth of the four evaporation/percolation ponds is five feet with two (2)
feet of freeboard.
9. Attachment B also shows the location of the trees and other plants that the Discharger
proposes to plant and irrigate with undisinfected secondary recycled water. The trees and
other plants that the Discharger plans to plant and irrigate are as follows:
a. Mesquite Trees;
b. Brittlebush;
c. Turtleback;
d. Dye-Weed;
e. Loco-Weed; and
f. Burrow-Weed.
