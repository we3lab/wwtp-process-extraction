2. The Discharger is discharging a maximum daily flow of 50,000 gallons-per-day
(gpd) of domestic sewage from 110 mobile home spaces and 647 campground
spaces. Thirty-four campground spaces are connected to the sewerage system
and the other 613 campground spaces do not generate wastewater. The
wastewater is pumped by one sewer lift station and discharged into two clay lined
oxidation basins for evaporation with a design average daily flow of 28,000 gpd of
effluent. A third basin used only for emergency conditions was designed as a
percolation basin and is to the north of the two oxidation basins. There was no
discharge to the third basin in 2015, and there has been no discharge to it in 2016,
so far. There is no flow metering or screening of the wastewater generated by the
Facility.

8. This Board Order regulates the entire wastewater collection, treatment and
disposal system, Wastewater Treatment Facility (WWTF) at Park Moabi Marina.
The WWTF at Park Moabi Marina consists of collection lines, septic tanks, holding
tanks, sewer lift station with pump lines and the evaporation basins, operated
independently, for disposal by evaporation. Solids that accumulate in the primary
septic tanks are periodically pumped by a licensed septage hauler for offsite
disposal at a facility permitted for that purpose.
9. Wastewater from the WWTF is discharged to one basin at a time; the receiving
basin is alternated to allow each basin to periodically dry up.
10. Annual precipitation in the vicinity averages about 4 inches per year.
11. The evaporation rate is about 84 inches per year and varyies by season, with
highest evaporation rates occurring during the months of May through August, and
the lowest during the months of November through February.
