4. Wastewater treatment at the Facility consists of a rotating screen for removal of large

trickling filter is typically only used during the summer. When the trickling filter is online,
approximately twenty percent of the influent flow is diverted from the aerated grit unit to
remove additional BOD and suspended solids from the wastewater before combining
with the remaining influent flow from the aerated grit unit and entering the aeration tanks.

maintain consistent compliance with the receiving water bacterial standards. Solids
