2. WASTEWATER SYSTEM OPERATION SUMMARY
In the early 2000s, the property experienced issues with recurrent effluent violations of
turbidity, total coliform, settleable solids, suspended solids, and biochemical oxygen
demand (BOD). Furthermore, the property experience issues with violations for multiple
incomplete reports. On January 24, 2008, The Central Coast Water Quality Control
Board issued a Cleanup and Abatement Order [CAO] (Order No. R3-2008-0022) to the
property owner. The property owner complied with the terms of the CAO and on May
19, 2011, the CAO was rescinded.
The treatment system is composed of conveyance piping, lift stations, a grinder, two
sequencing batch reactors, a decant tank, a sand filter, a chlorination system, a sludge
digestion and dewatering system, emergency storage pond, and a supervisory control
and data acquisition (SCADA) system. A summary of the system is included in Table 2
below. A facility site map and dispersal system layout are shown in Attachment 2.

6. EMERGENCY STORAGE POND REQUIREMENTS
The Cypress Ridge Sewer WWTP is equipped with an emergency storage pond for
temporarily storing wastewater if it does not meet the Water Recycling Criteria in
California Code of Regulations (CCR) title 22, division 4, chapter 3. Effluent wastewater
may be diverted to the emergency storage pond if the continuously monitored data

(Turbidity) provides indication of an imminent violation of the Water Recycling Criteria in
California Code of Regulations (CCR) title 22, division 4, chapter 3. The emergency
storage pond has capacity for 3 days of wastewater flow and three days of storm water
flow. Water stored in the pond will be pumped back to the headworks of the wastewater
treatment facility.
Cypress Ridge Sewer Co. is not authorized to discharge effluent wastewater to the
emergency unlined storage pond if the treated wastewater meets the Water Recycling
Criteria in title 22. Fully treated recycled water that meets the Water Recycling Criteria
must be discharged to the lined impoundments on the Cypress Ridge Golf Course.
Cypress Ridge Sewer Co. is only authorized to discharge effluent wastewater to the
emergency storage pond if the effluent quality does not meet the Water Recycling
Criteria. Wastewater discharged to the emergency storage pond must undergo
secondary treatment (i.e., oxidized wastewater) before being discharged to the pond.
Wastewater discharged without secondary treatment is considered a bypass and is a
violation. Cypress Ridge Sewer must operate the emergency storage pond to comply
with section B.5. of the General Permit. The wastewater discharged to the emergency
storage pond must comply with the effluent limit specified below (Table 6).Cypress
Ridge Sewer Co. is required to report any discharge to the emergency unlined storage
pond as described in the Monitoring and Reporting Program (Order No. R3-2023-0055).
