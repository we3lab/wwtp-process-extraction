4. The wastewatertreatment consists ofthe following:
a. Preliminarytreatment: bar screens and grit chambers,
b. Primarytreatment: primary clarification and primary sludge pumping,
c. Secondary treatment: activated sludge process, secondary clarification, sludge
recycling, and sludge wasting, and
d. Tertiarytreatment: coagulation, filtration, and chlorination/dechlorination.
5. Sludge and solids at CCWRF are pumped to and treated at the discharger's RP-2 facility
at a flow rate of about 1.2 mgd. CCWRF is designed to transfer and treat the recycled
flow from RP-2. Solids treatment includes gravity thickeners and dissolved air flotation
thickeners, anaerobic digestion, digester gas utilization, and belt press dewatering.
Dewatered sludge is hauled away for offsite disposal. The RP-2 solids treatment is
regulated under waste discharge requirements OrderNo. R8-2003-0003, issued to IEUA's
RP-5 treatment plant.
