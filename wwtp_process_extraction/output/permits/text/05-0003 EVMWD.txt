6. The wastewatertreatment consists ofthe following:
a. Preliminarytreatment consists ofbar screening and grit chambers.
b. Secondary treatment utilizes oxidation ditch technology and anaerobic reactors
for carbonaceous and nitrogenous pollutantreduction.
c. Tertiary treatment consists of monomediafiltration. Ultraviolet light is used for
disinfection.
d. Primary sludge treatment consists ofbeltpress mechanical dewatering and drying.
Sludge bed dewatering and drying is also employed, especially in summer.
7. The discharge outfall to Temescal Creek (Outfall 001) is located at latitude 37°27'00" and
longitude 117°21'00". The discharge outfall to Lake Elsinore (Outfall 002) is located at
latitude 33°40'45" and longitude 117°19'44".
8. The RWRF currently produces 4.66 mgd oftertiary treated wastewater. Currently, up to
3.35 mgd of wastewater is discharged to Reach 5, Temescal Creek, of which 0.5 mgd
flows in a channel alongside Temescal Creekthat goes into Gunerson Pond, a constructed
wetland. Up to 1.1 mgd flow is discharged to Lake Elsinore for lake water
replenishment. EVWMD plans to discharge up to 7..5 mgd recycled water to the Lake to
stabilizethe lake water elevation and thereby improve water quality and beneficial uses.
