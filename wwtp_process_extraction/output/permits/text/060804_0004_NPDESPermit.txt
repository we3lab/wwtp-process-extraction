II. FINDINGS
The California Regional Water Quality Control Board, North Coast Region (hereinafter Regional
Water Board), finds:
A. Background. The City of Cloverdale (hereinafter Discharger) is currently discharging under
Order No. 96-9 and National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) Permit No.
CA0022977, adopted on May 23, 1996. The Discharger submitted a Report of Waste Discharge,
dated April 24, 2002, and applied for a NPDES permit renewal to discharge up to 1.0 million
gallons per day of disinfected, secondary treated wastewater from Cloverdale Wastewater
Treatment Facility, hereinafter Facility. The application was deemed complete on May 24, 2002.
Supplemental information was requested from the Discharger on November 15, 2005 and
received on December 15, 2005.
B. Facility Description. The Discharger owns and operates a secondary municipal wastewater
treatment facility and associated collection system and disposal facilities. The Facility serves
approximately 3,000, primarily residential, connections within the City of Cloverdale. The
treatment system consists of a 2.8 million gallon primary aeration basin, secondary aeration, a
settling/polishing pond, and chlorination system. Disinfected, secondary effluent is discharged
from Discharge Point 002 to percolation ponds adjacent to the Russian River, a water of the
United States within the Russian River Watershed (Middle Russian River Hydrologic Area).
Sludge solids are retained in the aeration basins. Attachment B provides a topographic map of
the area around the Facility. Attachment C provides a flow schematic of the Facility.
