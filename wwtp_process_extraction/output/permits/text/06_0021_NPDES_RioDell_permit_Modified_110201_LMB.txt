II. FACILITY DESCRIPTION
A. Description of Wastewater and Biosolids Treatment or Controls
The City of Rio Dell owns and operates a collection and treatment system, which serves
approximately 1400 residential, commercial, and institutional users. The treatment
system consists of a flow-metering flume, primary clarification, secondary treatment by
rotating biological contactors, secondary clarification, and chlorination/dechlorination
capability. Waste sludge is aerobically digested and dried on sludge drying beds before
being land applied. Flow capacity is not a concern in dry summer months, but excessive
infiltration and inflow (I/I) during the winter can cause partially treated wastewater to
overflow the rotating batch reactors, flooding portions of the facility.
Wastewater is discharged from Discharge Point 001 to the Eel River, waters of the
United States, between October 1 and May 15 of each year. D
