19. Fillmore WRP Treatment Processes
A. The Fillmore WRP has a treatment capacity for an average daily flowrate of 1.8
million gallons per day (mgd) and a peak flowrate of 5.0 mgd. It will have a
treatment capacity for an average daily flowrate of 2.4 mgd and a peak flowrate
of 6.0 mgd for future expansion.
B. The Fillmore WRP wastewater treatment process consists of flow equalization,
preliminary treatment (coarse and fine screening and grit removal), secondary
anoxic and aerobic biological treatment for nitrate and biochemical chemical
oxygen (BOD) removal, tertiary treatment using membrane bioreactors (MBR)
for liquid/solids separation, and pathogen disinfection using ultraviolet (UV), as
shown in Figure 3.
20. Fillmore WRP Effluent Quality
A. The Fillmore WRP produces disinfected tertiary recycled water and discharges
to onsite and offsite disposal areas and recycled water use areas for landscape
irrigation, construction dust control, soil compaction, and demonstration
wetland applications.
