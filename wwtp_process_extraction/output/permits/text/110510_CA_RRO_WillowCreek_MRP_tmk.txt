The treatment system will provide secondary treatment with nitrogen removal.
The total maximum sewage design flow is estimated to be 8,100 gallons per day,
based on an estimated 150 gallon per day per bedroom.
This Order includes monitoring and reporting requirements in Monitoring and
Reporting Program Order No. R1-2011-0061. Order No. R1-2011-0061 requires
surface inspections of the leachfield areas, effluent monitoring, collection of
groundwater level data, and groundwater sampling downgradient of the leachfield
areas.
