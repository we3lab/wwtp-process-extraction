The new domestic OWTS will receive waste from the commercial kitchen, the tasting
room and bathrooms. The system will consist of a 15,000 gallon grease trap, grinder
pump/lift station, 5,000 gallon recirculation tank, two Advantex filtration systems, a
2,000 gallon sump tank and a sub-surface drip disposal field. The OWTS has been
designed to treat and dispose of a peak wastewater flow rate of 5,585 gallons per day
(GPD). The sub-surface drip disposal field will be located in the vineyard north of the
Advantex treatment systems and will be composed of two separate disposal zones.
The septic tank/leach field system that currently treats domestic waste at the Facility will
be decommissioned following installation and operation of the new OWTS.
