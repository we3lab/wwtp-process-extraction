The Permittee owns and operates a wastewater treatment and disposal system comprised
of a gravity collection system, Imhoff tank, and percolation/evaporation ponds. The
Facility has six percolation/evaporation ponds for effluent disposal.
The collection system consists of a conventional gravity collection system with two small
lift stations which transport wastewater to the Imhoff tank. Wastewater from the Imhoff
tank flows to a 2.6-acre pond system for treatment and disposal.
