II. FACILITY DESCRIPTION
The Permittee owns and operates the Facility, a domestic wastewater facility that serves a population
of approximately 7,000 within the City of Fort Bragg and surrounding unincorporated areas. The
Facility is located on 101 West Cypress Street in Fort Bragg, Mendocino County, California.
A. Description of Wastewater and Biosolids Treatment and Controls
The treatment facility has an average dry weather design treatment capacity of 1.0 mgd, and an
average wet weather treatment capacity of 2.2 mgd. The wastewater treatment plant consists of
rag screening, grit removal, primary clarification, two‐stage trickling filters, secondary
clarification, chlorination using gaseous chlorine and sulfur dioxide dechlorination. Effluent is
discharged through a diffuser at Discharge Point 001 to the Pacific Ocean.
Sludge from the primary clarifier is pumped to a gravity thickener and then to two anaerobic
digesters. Skimmings from the primary clarifier are sent to an unlined grease lagoon for drying
and storage, and then are taken to a municipal solid waste landfill for final disposal. Sludge from
the secondary clarifier is pumped back to the primary clarifier and then the commingled sludge is
pumped to the sludge thickener before being conveyed to the primary digester. The digested
primary and secondary settled sludge is dewatered to a minimum of 15 percent solids in a
gravity belt press. Additional sludge drying takes place in sludge drying beds prior to landfill
disposal.
B. Discharge Points and Receiving Waters
Treated wastewater is discharged west of the Facility at Discharge Point 001 at 39° 26’ 20” N
latitude and 123° 48’ 48” W longitude to the Pacific Ocean via a 650 foot outfall.
