3. A Report of Waste Discharge (ROWD) for renewal of waste discharge
requirements was requested by December 31, 2014, for the discharge up to 0.130
mgd (ADWF) of treated wastewater from the City of Etna Wastewater Treatment
Facility (WWTF), hereinafter Facility. No ROWD was submitted.
The Discharger owns and operates a wastewater treatment and disposal system
comprised of a gravity collection system, and oxidation/percolation/evaporation
ponds. The Facility has five oxidation ponds for treatment and three
percolation/evaporation ponds for effluent disposal.
The collection system consists of a conventional gravity collection system, which
transports wastewater to the pond system for treatment and disposal. There is no
screening and no removal of solids prior to wastewater entering the oxidation
ponds.
