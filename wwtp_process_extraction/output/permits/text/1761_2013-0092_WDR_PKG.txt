longitude of 119° 08' 48.44" (Figure 2). An unlined flood control channel, referred to as

20. The Jose Flores WWTP is a secondary wastewater treatment plant. The treatment
process starts at the headworks where wastewater is passed through an auger monster
to remove trash. Wastewater is then pumped to the sequencing batch reactors for
biological treatment. The treated effluent is discharged to evaporation/percolation ponds
located along the north bank of the Santa Clara River.
21. Sludge from the sequencing batch reactor is pumped into an aerobic digester for primary
stabilization and then is transferred into the Geotube® for separation of water from the
biosolids. The Geotube® is a porous bag that allows for storage, containment, and
dewatering of biosolids. When the bags are full and sufficiently dry they are opened and
the dried biosolids are hauled to the landfill for disposal.
