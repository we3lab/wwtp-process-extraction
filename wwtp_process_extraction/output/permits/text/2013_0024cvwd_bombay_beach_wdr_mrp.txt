4. The WRP consists of two mechanically aerated concrete-lined oxidation basins, two unlined
stabilization basins, and six evaporation/percolation ponds. Final disposal of treated
wastewater is by evaporation-percolation in the basins. The WRP has a reported design
treatment capacity of 150,000 gallons per day (gpd).
5. During the period of 1991 through 2011, average annual wastewater flows from the WRP
were 15.426 million gallons (MG). Annual wastewater discharges have declined steadily
from 26.819 MG in 1998 to 7.495 MG in 2011.
6. Disposal ponds are checked annually for sludge accumulation and dredged as necessary.
7. Back-up power is available by portable generators.
8. There are no significant industrial users discharging to the WRP’s collection system.

9. Annual precipitation averages about three (3) inches. Annual evapotranspiration rate is
approximately 72 inches.
