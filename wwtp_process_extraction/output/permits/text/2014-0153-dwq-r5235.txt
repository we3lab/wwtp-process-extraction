SUBJECT: REVIEW OF NITRATE AND SETBACK CONDITIONS FOR GOLDEN MEADOWS
COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICT, GENERAL ORDER WQ 2014-0153-DWQ-
R5235, TEHAMA COUNTY
I have reviewed the case file for Golden Meadows Community Services District (Golden
Meadows CSD) and performed an inspection on 5 May 2017. The file and inspection
document the general condition of the wastewater treatment and subsurface disposal
system. The Discharger has kept adequate maintenance documentation and all treatment
and collection infrastructure appears in good order.
The average daily wastewater flow is approximately 2,000 gallons per day.
Potential Threats to Water Quality
The wastewater treatment system is located just northeast of the Hwy 36 West, Red Bluff
Tehama County. The closest distance from the leachfield to the nearest property line is
greater than 5 feet. The closest potable water well is greater than 150 feet. The closest
surface water is greater than 500 feet from leachfield. A monitoring well network consisting
of 3 wells was installed at the site due to high groundwater and poor soil conditions.
Completion of the Nitrate Checklist in Attachment 1 of Order 2014-0153-DWQ indicates the
following flow and rationale:
A1 Exceed 20,000 gpd? No.
Wastewater flow generally less than 2,000 gpd.
Conclusion: No nitrogen removal is required.
Monitoring Requirements
To protect water quality, General Order monitoring requirements will be sufficient. In
summary, Staff recommends quarterly reporting of the average daily flow rate; quarterly
leach field monitoring, quarterly groundwater monitoring of wells MW-1 through MW-3 and
annual inspections of the septic tank. Quarterly monitoring will be reported by the first day of
the second month after the quarter ends (e.g. January-March report is due by May 1st).
Annual monitoring will be included with the fourth quarter monitoring.
