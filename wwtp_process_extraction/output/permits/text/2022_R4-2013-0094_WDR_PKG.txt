===PLANNED CHANGES===

Planned Changes
The Discharger has completed the construction of an AWPF. The GREAT Program - Phase I is
designed to produce 6.25 mgd of recycled water. Phase II is designed to produce an additional
25 mgd of recycled water. Recycled water is produced by further processing a portion of the
secondary-treated wastewater from the Oxnard WWTP through microfiltration, ultrafiltration,
reverse osmosis, and ultraviolet-light-based advanced oxidation.
