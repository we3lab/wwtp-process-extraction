1. The Saugus WRP uses the following process sequence: Comminution, grit removal,
primary sedimentation, flow equalization, activated sludge biological treatment with
nitrification and denitrification, secondary clarification, inert media filtration,
chlorination and dechlorination. Treated wastewater discharged to the Santa Clara
River is dechlorinated. Effluent delivered for reuse is not dechlorinated.
2. No facilities are provided for solids processing at the plant. A portion of the raw
sewage from the tributary area, together with primary sludge and primary
skimmings, is bypassed and conveyed via an interceptor sewer to the Valencia WRP
for treatment and disposal. All waste activated sludge separated from the
wastewater is transported, via a sludge force main, to the Valencia WRP where
treatment occurs under Order No. 95-081 (NPDES No. CA0054216).

Planned Changes
The last major upgrade at the Saugus WRP was the Saugus NitrificationIDenitrification
Facilities, which were completed in September 2003. Since then, there have been only
minor upgrades including paving repairs and improvements to the primary tank
, skimmers.
As part of its effort to comply with the Upper Santa Clara River chloride Total Maximum
Daily Load (TMDL), the SCVSD is considering conversion to Ultra-Violet Light (UV)
Disinfection technology, in order to help achieve compliance with the TMDL by avoiding
the addition of chlorine; a design schedule has not yet been established.
