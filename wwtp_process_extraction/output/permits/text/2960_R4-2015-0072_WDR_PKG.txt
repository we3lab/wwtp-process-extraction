===PLANNED CHANGES===

Planned Changes
The Saugus WRP’s treatment system had been upgraded in the past to nitrify and de-nitrify
the effluent for ammonia-nitrogen and nitrate-nitrogen removal. Major plant upgrades are
proposed by the Permittee for the Saugus WRP to achieve compliance with the Upper Santa
Clara River Chloride TMDL. SCVSD proposes to implement the EIR alternative which
includes construction of UV disinfection facilities at both the Saugus and Valencia WRPs,
coupled with membrane filtration/reverse osmosis (MF/RO) facilities and brine disposal via
deep well injection (DWI) at the Valencia WRP. These facilities are necessary to attain the
chloride numeric target in Reach 5 of the Santa Clara River, downstream of the WRPs.
