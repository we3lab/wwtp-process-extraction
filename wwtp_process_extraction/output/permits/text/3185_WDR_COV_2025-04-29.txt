The Facility is designed to treat up to 40,000 gallons per day (gpd) of domestic
wastewater with a package treatment system consisting of a comminutor/bar screen, an
anoxic basin, dual aerobic basins, dual clarifiers, and sodium hypochlorite disinfection
contact chamber, and irrigation water storage pond, as shown in Figure 2. The disinfected
water meeting Title 22 secondary-23 recycled water criteria is conveyed to a bentonite-
lined 1.5-million-gallon recycled water storage pond and distributed to hillside slope
irrigation areas around the perimeter of the property (Figure 3). Disinfected secondary-23
recycled water requires a 7-day median concentration of total coliform less than 23 most
probable number per 100 milliliters (MPN/100 mL) and must not exceed 240 MPN/100
mL in more than one sample in any 30-day period.
