FACILITY SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS AND EFFLUENT LIMITATIONS
The Discharger will maintain exclusive control over the discharge and shall comply with the
terms and conditions of this NOA, General Order 2014-0153-DWQ-R5296, with all attachments,
and MRP No. 2014-0153-DWQ-R5296.
In accordance with Section B.1 of the General Order, treated wastewater discharged to the
leach field system shall not exceed a monthly average daily discharge of 6,000 gpd. In
accordance with the requirements of the General Order this NOA does not specify a nitrogen
effluent limitation since the Facility flow rate is less than 20,000 gpd.
The General Order states in Section B.1 that the Discharger shall comply with the setbacks as
described in Table 3 of the General Order. This table summarizes different setback
requirements for wastewater treatment system equipment, activities, land application areas, and
storage and/or treatment ponds from sensitive receptors and property lines where applicable.
