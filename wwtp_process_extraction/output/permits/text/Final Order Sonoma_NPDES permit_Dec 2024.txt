Planned Upgrades. The Discharger plans to complete the following projects
during this permit term. These changes are mentioned here for informational
purposes only and are not requirements of this Order, except to the extent that
they pertain to ensuring Facility reliability. Nevertheless, this Order authorizes
these projects. Even so, the Discharger may need to obtain other necessary
permits or permit modifications to implement these changes.
The Discharger is currently constructing its influent/effluent pumping and piping

future dechlorination and disinfection practices due to its sulfur dioxide gas

the previous order term to prevent the discharge of chlorinated effluent to receiving
waters (see Fact Sheet section 6.3.5.6).
