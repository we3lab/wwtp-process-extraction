2. WASTEWATER SYSTEM OPERATION SUMMARY
The City of Guadalupe wastewater treatment facility (Wastewater System) is a Class III
wastewater system. The City of Guadalupe must staff a Grade III chief plant operator for
proper operation of the Wastewater System.
The Wastewater System receives septage from third party septic tank hauling
companies and domestic and industrial wastewater from the City of Guadalupe. The
Wastewater System is capable of treating up to 660,000 gallons per day (gpd) of
wastewater to a secondary treatment level and up to 960,000 gpd to undisinfected
secondary recycled7 water quality. The Wastewater System consists of the treatment
technologies and treatment capabilities shown in Table 2.

Treatment train no. 1 is a Swanson advanced integrated
wastewater pond system (AIWPS) that consists of one primary
facultative pond (Pond 2, volume = 5.2 million gallons) and one
secondary aerobic pond (Pond 4, volume = 2.2 million gallons).
Pond 4 can recirculate about 25 percent of the daily inflow back
to Pond 2 in the AIWPS system during daylight hours. A 5HP
recirculation pump operating at 415 gallons per minute achieves
this design parameter. Pond 2 must be equipped with 50 HP of
surface aeration (two at 15HP and one at 20 HP) and Pond 4
Secondary must be equipped with 10 HP of surface aeration (one at 10HP).
Treatment Train The AIWPS is designed to produce effluent concentration of 5-
No. 1 day biochemical oxygen demand (BOD) at 25 milligrams per liter
(mg/L) and total suspended solids at 100 mg/L, when operating
in stabilized mode. Pond aeration is required to maintain a
dissolved oxygen level at about 2.0 mg/L to suppress odors.
This treatment train produces a much higher quality of effluent
than a typical primary wastewater treatment system and is
considered secondary treatment but it is not designed to
produce a level of secondary treated wastewater as defined in
the Code of Federal Regulations title 40, chapter I, subchapter
D, part 133 section 133.1028.
