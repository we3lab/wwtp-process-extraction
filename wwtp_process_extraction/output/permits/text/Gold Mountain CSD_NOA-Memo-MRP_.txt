Potential Threats to Water Quality
The wastewater treatment system is located southwest of the main community of Portola.
The closest distance from the subsurface discharge fields (leachfields) to the nearest
property line is greater than 50 feet. The closest potable water well is greater than 500 feet
from the leachfields. The closest surface water is greater than 500 feet from the leachfields.
A monitoring well network was installed at the site due to high groundwater, poor soil
conditions and its proximity to Middle Fork Feather River. Completion of the Nitrate Checklist
in Attachment 1 of Order 2014-0153-DWQ indicates the following flow and rationale:
A1 Exceed 20,000 gpd? No.
Wastewater flow generally less than 9,000 gpd.
Conclusion: No nitrogen removal is required.

Monitoring Requirements
To protect water quality, General Order monitoring requirements will be sufficient. In
summary, Staff recommends quarterly reporting of the average daily flow rate; quarterly
leach field monitoring, quarterly groundwater monitoring and annual inspections of the septic
tank. Quarterly monitoring will be reported by the first day of the second month after the
quarter ends (e.g. January-March report is due by May 1st). Annual monitoring will be
included with the fourth quarter monitoring.
