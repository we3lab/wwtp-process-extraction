2.2. Groundwater
Jalama Beach does not overlie a groundwater basin as defined by the California
Department of Water Resources Bulletin 118 or the Water Quality Control Plan
for the Central Coastal Basin (Basin Plan).4 Past subsurface investigations
around the Jalama Beach County Park have encountered groundwater at
approximately 12 feet below ground surface at the playground, Area A, and Area
B leachfields. The encountered water is presumed to be ocean water underneath
the backshore.
2.3. Pacific Ocean
The Pacific Ocean is west of Jalama Beach County Park. The ocean’s high tide
line is approximately 150 feet west of the septic tanks that serve the restaurant,
the northwest small restroom, and the southwest large restroom. It is
approximately 200 feet west of leachfields Area A and Area B. The septic tanks
and leachfileds are within the area called the backshore.
