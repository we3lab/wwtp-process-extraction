The Facility is located at 35250 Mather Road, Groveland in Tuolumne County,
approximately 12 miles east of Groveland, and 10 miles northeast of Highway 120. The
Facility consists of a small septic tank and a leach field which is utilized during the
winter (Winter OWTS), and a larger septic system connected to five leach fields utilized
during the summer (Summer OWTS). For majority of the year, there is only one staff
member at the camp. During such times, typical wastewater flow is about 350 gpd.
