2. The City of Tulelake (hereinafter the Permittee) owns wastewater collection and
treatment facilities serving the community. The WWTF is designed to treat 0.16
million gallon per day (mgd) of wastewater to secondary treatment standards.
WWTF treatment components include headworks, two aerated lagoons, two sand
filters, and chlorination and dechlorination chambers. The headworks include an
on-site pump station receiving sewage from the collection system, comminutor,
and pumps. Treated wastewater is disinfected using chlorine gas and
dechlorinated with sulfur dioxide. Treated effluent is discharged through
discharge Serial No. 001 into Tulelake Irrigation District Drain No. 44-B-1 at a
point of latitude 41°56’55” North and longitude 121°28’15” West, located within
the NE ¼ of Section 2, T47N, R4E, MDB&M (Map A Appendix 1, herein made
part of this Order).
