2. Treatment. The Facility receives wastewater that has already been treated to secondary
treatment standards though screening, primary sedimentation, activated sludge, secondary
clarification, and chlorination from the Dublin San Ramon Services District and City of
Livermore wastewater treatment plants. Typically, this treated wastewater is discharged
through the EBDA pipeline. Prior to any necessary wet weather discharge to San Lorenzo
Creek or Alamo Canal, the wastewater is dechlorinated. Attachment C provides a flow
schematic.
3. Stormwater. The Discharger is not required to be covered under the statewide NPDES
permit for stormwater discharges associated with industrial activities (NPDES General
Permit No. CAS000001) because all stormwater flows in contact with equipment or
wastewater at the Facility are collected and directed to the headworks of the Dublin San
Ramon Services District Wastewater Treatment Plant for treatment.
B. Discharge Point and Receiving Water
This Order covers two intermittent wet weather discharge points for secondary-treated, disinfected,
and dechlorinated municipal wastewater. These discharges occur in San Lorenzo Creek (Discharge
Point No. 002) and Alamo Canal (Discharge Point No. 003), a constructed flood control channel.
The wet weather outfall to San Lorenzo Creek is a shallow water outfall that does not have a
diffuser. The discharge location is approximately 3,000 feet upstream of Lower San Francisco Bay.
