1. Location and Service Area. The wastewater treatment plant is located at 3700 Paradise
Drive in Tiburon. It provides secondary treatment of domestic wastewater for Sanitary
District No. 5 in Marin County. The Facility serves a population of about 400 through 109
service connections. Attachment B shows a map of the area around the Facility.
2. Collection System. The collection system is a separate sewer system comprised of
approximately 3.5 miles of publicly-owned pipes.
3. Wastewater Treatment. The plant can provide secondary treatment for an average daily dry
weather design flow of 0.04 million gallons per day (MGD). In 2014, the average daily flow
was 0.015 MGD. The wastewater treatment process uses two identical treatment trains, each
with an average daily dry weather capacity of 0.02 MGD, consisting of grinding, influent
screening, flow equalization (primary clarification), extended aeration, secondary
clarification, chlorination (sodium hypochlorite), and dechlorination (sodium bisulfite). The
Discharger plans to replace its chlorination and dechlorination disinfection system with an
ultraviolet (UV) light system. Chlorine disinfection and dechlorination will be used if the UV
disinfection system is out of service.
4. Sludge and Biosolids Management. Sludge is stored onsite in a storage tank prior to being
pumped and transported to the Sanitary District No. 5, Tiburon Wastewater Treatment Plant for
treatment and disposal.
