II. FACILITY DESCRIPTION
The Discharger provides sewerage service for the University of California, Davis campus
and serves a population of approximately 45,000. The WWTP design daily average flow
capacity is 3.6 mgd.
A. Description of Wastewater and Biosolids Treatment or Controls
The treatment system at the Facility consists of a communitor, mechanical bar screen,
oxidation ditch activated sludge process, secondary clarifiers, filtration, and ultraviolet
light disinfection. Biosolids treatment consists of solids stabilization basins and sludge
drying beds. Each summer, stabilized sludge is pumped into the drying beds for
dewatering and dewatered biosolids are transported and disposed at local Class II
landfill as daily cover.
