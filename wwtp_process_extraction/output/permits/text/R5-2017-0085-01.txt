A. Eligible Discharges
This General Order applies to owners and operators (hereafter Dischargers) of municipal
wastewater treatment facilities that meet water quality objectives/criteria at the point of
discharge to surface waters of the United States. These facilities may be publicly owned
treatment works (POTW’s), as defined at 40 C.F.R. section 403.3, or privately owned
treatment works, as defined at 40 C.F.R. section 122.2. This General Order covers major and
minor discharges and does not specify eligibility criteria for flow.
The municipal wastewater treatment facilities to be covered by this General Order receive and
treat primarily municipal and domestic sewage (i.e., waste and wastewater from humans or
household operations), but may also receive and treat septage, commercial and industrial
wastewater, storm water, and dry-weather diversions from municipal separate storm sewer
(MS4) systems. This General Order does not authorize discharges to surface waters directly
from septic tanks or discharges that are comprised solely of non-municipal wastewater (e.g.,
commercial wastewater, industrial wastewater, or storm water).
