The tertiary treatment system at the Facility consists of the following:
· A tertiary package treatment system consisting of two identical parallel treatment
trains with:
o Anoxic Basins
o Aeration Basins
o Immersed Membrane Bioreactor (MBR) treatment system, consisting of:
· influent headworks with course screening followed by fine screening;
· biological nutrient removal processes where BOD reduction, nitrogen
removal, and phosphorus removal occurs;
· a series of permeate pumps that pull the wastestream through the
membranes by vacuum provides solids separation; and
· disinfection includes two parallel ultraviolet light (UV) systems.
· Permeate pumps to pull effluent through filters
· Two inline ultraviolet light disinfection units
· One emergency storage pond
· Three reclamation ponds
Biosolids:
Scum and waste activated sludge produced from waste material in the MBR tanks
is digested in aerobic sludge digesters followed by centrifuge dewatering. The
dewatered sludge is taken off-site for disposal in a landfill.
