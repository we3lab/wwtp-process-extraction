4. The wastewater treatment plant consists of preliminary and secondary treatment, and
solids and effluent disposal systems. The process flow is shown in Attachment B
(Process Flow Diagram), made part of this Board Order by reference.
5. Untreated wastewater flows to the preliminary treatment system, which consists of a bar
screened by-pass channel, one channel grinder, one comminutor and a gravity grit
collection box. Wastewater from the preliminary treatment system gravity flows to one of
two oxidation ditch units with integral clarifiers for secondary (biological) treatment. Each
oxidation ditch unit has a rated treatment capacity of 0.09 MGD. The effluent from the
secondary treatment system flows to one of three evaporation/inflitration ponds for
disposal.
6. Scum and sludge wasting from the integral clarifiers are pumped to one of the nine sludge-
drying beds. The scum, sludge, grit and screenings collected are transported to the Alan
L. Horton Wastewater Treatment Plant located in Desert Hot Springs, for disposal with its
sludge, grit and screenings. A private contractor hauls away the combined solids to a land
fill or a private composting facility.

Hydrogeologic Conditions
8. Annual precipitation in the region averages about 5 inches. Annual evapotranspiration
rate in the vicinity is approximately 60 inches.
9. There are no surface waters in the vicinity of the evaporation/percolation ponds. An
ephemeral wash borders the southwest corner of the WWTP.
10. There are no domestic wells within 1,000 feet of the on-site evaporation/percolation ponds.
There are some groundwater wells in the area are used for hot mineral spas.
11. The Discharger reports that the depth to groundwater is 180 feet.
12. The Discharger reports that the soil profile in the area of the facility consists of sandy soil,
fine to coarse, clean angular whitish gray and dry. Some boulders to 18 inches and rocks
to nine inches. The soils in the area typically have high a infiltration rate.
