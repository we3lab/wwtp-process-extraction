II. FACILITY DESCRIPTION
A. Wastewater and Biosolids Treatment or Controls
The wastewater treatment system consists of bar screens, activated sludge extended
aeration, equalization, clarification, dual media sand filtration, UV disinfection and
sludge thickening/drying. The Facility produces tertiary treated water that complies with
requirements established in Title 22 of the California Code of Regulations for
unrestricted non-potable water reuse. The Discharger proposes to add a reverse
osmosis treatment system to meet the TDS and TIN requirements of this Order.
The Facility utilizes sludge thickeners and a centrifuge to dewater biosolids.
Stormwater runoff from the facility is discharged to Cooper's Creek through four
separate outfalls.
