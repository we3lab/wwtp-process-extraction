D. Compliance Summary
Completion of Secondary Treatment Projects
Since the construction of the facilities that was required to achieve secondary
treatment standards were not completed at the time the previous permit was issued,
OC San entered into a consent decree mandating OC San to meet secondary
treatment limits by December 31, 2012. After extensive process improvement and
Capital Improvement Projects (CIP) including completion of the following:
(cid:120) Trickling filter project at Reclamation Plant No. 1, completed in Spring 2006;
(cid:120) Secondary treatment rehabilitation project at Treatment Plant No. 2, completed
in Spring 2008;
(cid:120) New trickling filters and solid contact basins at Treatment Plant No. 2,
completed in Summer 2011. The project added 60 MGD of secondary
treatment capacity; and
(cid:120) New activated sludge system at Reclamation Plant No. 1, completed in Fall
2012. The project added 60 MGD of secondary treatment capacity.
The Discharger has officially declared the POTW at full secondary treatment on
December 28, 2012 and continue operating at and meeting full secondary
requirements.

Discharger started to note negative changes in the structure and health of benthic

available for later use, if determined necessary. Use of disinfection remains at the
other two discharge points (Discharge Points 002 and 003) to protect human health.
