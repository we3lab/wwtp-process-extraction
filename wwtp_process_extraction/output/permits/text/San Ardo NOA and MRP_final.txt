2), and an emergency overflow pond (Pond 3). A summary of the system is included in
Table 2 below. A facility site map and dispersal system layout are shown in Attachment

Wastewater flows from collection system to the lift station in
San Ardo. From the lift station, wastewater flows across the
Cattleman Road bridge via the sewer line and enters the
ponds.
Treatment / Disposal San Ardo Water District has two treatment/disposal oxidation
ponds (Pond 1 and Pond 2) for wastewater percolation and
evaporation. [1]
