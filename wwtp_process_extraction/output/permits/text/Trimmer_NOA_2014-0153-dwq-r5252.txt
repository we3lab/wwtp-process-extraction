DISCHARGE DESCRIPTION
The Facility is in Fresno County, approximately 1,200 feet east of the intersection of East
Trimmer Springs Road and Maxon Road (septic tank location at a point latitude 36.91o North,
and longitude 119.29o West). The site is approximately 5.4 miles north-northeast of the Pine
Flat Dam.
[Page 1]
Alexander Kan - 2 - 14 February 2018
US Army Corps of Engineers
The Facility consists of a septic system connected to two lined evaporation ponds. The Facility
receives domestic wastewater from one, six fixture restroom with hot showers. The average
daily flow in the treatment system is 125 gpd. The Facility is designed for a maximum average
flow rate of 36,000 gpd.
