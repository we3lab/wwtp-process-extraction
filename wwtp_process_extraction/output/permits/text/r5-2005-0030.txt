3. The treatment system consists of bar screening; grit removal; biological treatment in an
oxidation ditch and unlined, aerated equalization pond(s), including nitrification; secondary
sedimentation; coagulation and filtration; disinfection; and dechlorination. Sludge is dewatered
with a belt filter press and removed to a landfill.
4. The Report of Waste Discharge describes the wastewater discharge to Auburn Ravine as follows:
Design Average Dry Weather Flow Rate: 1.67 million gallons per day (mgd)
Annual Average Daily Flow Rate: 1.34 mgd
Maximum Daily Flow Rate: 5.67 mgd
Average Temperature, Summer: 73.8 (23.2) ºF (ºC)
Average Temperature, Winter: 57.6 (14.2) ºF (ºC)
Average Daily Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD): 6.7 mg/l
