II. FACILITY DESCRIPTION
The Discharger provides sewerage service for the Grizzly Ranch Development and is
designed to serve a population of approximately 380 single family homes, and an 18-hole
golf course. The design flow capacity of the Facility is 0.081 million gallons per day (mgd).
Currently, not enough homes are occupied for the facility to operate and startup has been
delayed. Therefore, wastewater from the Grizzly Ranch development is transported to an
alternative wastewater treatment facility in Portola. In 2010, the development generated
approximately 3,000 gallons to 5,000 gallons of wastewater per day.
A. Description of Wastewater and Biosolids Treatment or Controls
The Grizzly Ranch wastewater treatment system consists of a headworks with flow
metering and a rotary drum screen for grit and large solids removal; a sequencing batch
reactor for waste strength reduction and nitrogen removal; multi-media filters for
additional removal of biochemical oxygen demand, total suspended solids, and turbidity;
an emergency storage pond; an irrigation storage pond; an outfall diffuser in Big Grizzly
Creek; and a pumping station and irrigation distribution piping for recycled water
irrigation. Dried biosolids are hauled to a landfill.
