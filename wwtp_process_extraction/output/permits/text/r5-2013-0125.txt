II. FACILITY DESCRIPTION
The Discharger owns and operates two separate wastewater collection systems, one
to collect municipal wastewater and another to collect industrial wastewater. The
municipal wastewater collection system has 23,000 service laterals and consists of
178 miles of collection mains, 2,880 manholes, 7 lift stations, and 5 miles of trunk line
delivering wastewater to the Facility for treatment and disposal. Dischargers to the
domestic trunk line include a present population of approximately 63,500, businesses,
and some industries within the City of Lodi. Flows from the San Joaquin Flag City
Service Area are included in the City’s municipal flows.
