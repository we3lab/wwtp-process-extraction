II. FACILITY DESCRIPTION
The Discharger provides sewerage service for the City of Lincoln and western Placer County and
serves a population of approximately 43,000. The current design daily average dry weather flow
capacity of the Facility is 4.2 MGD.
A. Description of Wastewater and Sewage sludge Treatment and Controls
The treatment system consists of screening, oxidation ditches with anoxic zones, secondary
clarification, maturation ponds, dissolved air flotation (DAF), coagulation, flocculation, sand
filtration, and ultraviolet light (UV) disinfection. Sludge is dewatered using a belt filter press.
Dried sludge is hauled to a landfill. The Facility also includes a lined emergency storage basin
and two tertiary storage basins.
Tertiary treated effluent is either discharged to Auburn Ravine Creek at Discharge Point 001,

Order R5-2005-0040-01, as amended by Order R5-2012-0052). The Discharger has the

===PLANNED CHANGES===

Planned Changes
During the term of this Order, the Discharger is planning to expand and upgrade the Facility to
increase the design average dry weather flow from 4.2 MGD up to 5.9 MGD to accommodate
regionalization with the Placer County Sewer Maintenance District 1 (SMD1) Wastewater
Treatment Plant (WWTP). The regionalization project is proposed to be complete by
1 September 2015 in accordance with Cease and Desist Order (CDO) R5-2010-0093-01 for
the Placer County SMD1 WWTP. The existing plant capacity will be increased through the
addition of the following units:
1. Two influent pumps and additional headworks improvements;
2. One anoxic basin and oxidation ditch complex;
3. Two secondary clarifiers;
4. Modifications to the maturation ponds’ effluent structures;
5. Two filter cells;
6. Effluent pumping station improvements.
This Order allows for an increase in the permitted average dry weather flow to 5.9 MGD upon
completion of the regionalization project. This Order also allows an increase in the permitted
average dry weather flow up to 8.4 MGD (or some smaller increase) to accommodate growth
