II. FACILITY DESCRIPTION
The Discharger provides sewerage service for the City of Williams and serves a population of
approximately 5,100. The design average dry weather flow capacity of the Facility is 0.5 MGD.
A. Description of Wastewater and Biosolids Treatment and Controls
The Discharger completed an upgrade to the Facility during the term of Order
R5-2008-0185-01, which began operating in June 2011. The upgraded treatment system at
the Facility consists of an aeration basin, secondary clarifier, cloth filters, ultraviolet light (UV)
disinfection, and re-aeration basin. Sludge is dewatered using a belt filter press. Dewatered
solids from the belt filter press may be spread on a paved solids solar drying pad in the
northwest corner of the Facility site for additional drying. Dried biosolids are hauled to the
Ostrom Road Landfill in Wheatland, CA.
B. Discharge Points and Receiving Waters
1. The Facility is located in Section 11, T15N, R3W, MDB&M, as shown in Attachment B, a
part of this Order.
2. Treated municipal wastewater is discharged at Discharge Point 001 to Salt Creek, a
water of the United States, and a tributary to the Sacramento River via Freshwater Creek
and the Colusa Basin Drain at a point latitude 39° 10’ 11
