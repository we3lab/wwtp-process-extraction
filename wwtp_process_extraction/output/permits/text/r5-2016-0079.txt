6. The WWTF features secondary treatment and consists of a wet well with a grinder, two lift
pumps, an aeration basin, clarifier, six evaporation/percolation ponds, emergency basin, two
concrete lined sludge drying beds, and surrounding bermed land owned by the District which has
been utilized for discharge in the past during pond maintenance activities. A current site map is
included in Attachment A, which is attached hereto and made part of this Order by reference.
